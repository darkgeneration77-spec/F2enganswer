Question 1

Source / 原文： Bar chart: Watch Movie is the highest bar; Chat online is higher than Play online games; Reading book is the lowest category shown.
Which of the following is true about the chart above?
I. Less students chat online than play computer games.
II. The least favourite pastime is going shopping.
III. Most of the students like to watch movies.
Answer / 答案： III — Most of the students like to watch movies.
Key clue / 关键线索： Watch Movie has the longest bar.
Why this answer? / 为什么？
The longest bar represents the most preferred pastime, so Watch Movie is the correct choice.
最长的柱代表最多学生选择的活动，因此 Watch Movie 是正确答案。
Why not the other options? / 为什么其他选项不对？
I. The chart shows more students chat online than play online games.
II. Going shopping is not shown in the chart.
Reading skill / 阅读技巧： Compare bar length first: highest, lowest, then relationships.

Question 2

Source / 原文： Sign: “DO NOT HANDLE THE FRUIT. PLEASE WAIT TO BE SERVED.”
The sign means
I. Customers have to serve themselves.
II. Customers should not touch the fruits.
III. Customers are requested to serve each other.
Answer / 答案： II — Customers should not touch the fruits.
Key clue / 关键线索： DO NOT HANDLE THE FRUIT
Why this answer? / 为什么？
“Handle” here means touch or pick up. Customers must wait for service.
handle 在这里表示触摸或拿取，所以顾客不应该碰水果，而是等待服务。
Why not the other options? / 为什么其他选项不对？
I. The sign says customers should wait to be served, not serve themselves.
III. It does not ask customers to serve one another.
Reading skill / 阅读技巧： Command words: do not = prohibition（禁止）.

Question 3

Source / 原文： POWER FITNESS CLUB advert: “Email powerfitness@gmail.com to get further information.”
The public can get more information about the fitness club through
I. email
II. website
III. brochure
Answer / 答案： I — email
Key clue / 关键线索： Email ... to get further information
Why this answer? / 为什么？
The advert directly states the contact method.
广告直接写明通过 email 获取更多资料。
Why not the other options? / 为什么其他选项不对？
II. No website is provided.
III. No brochure is mentioned as the contact method.
Reading skill / 阅读技巧： Locate contact-method words exactly.

Question 4

Source / 原文： Email: Selangor was at an unhealthy API level, while other locations were close to unhealthy. “Luckily, the air quality and visibility improved after a heavy downpour.”
From the email, we know that the air quality
I. was unhealthy in all areas
II. remained unchanged
III. was improving
Answer / 答案： III — was improving
Key clue / 关键线索： air quality and visibility improved
Why this answer? / 为什么？
The word “improved” shows the air quality was getting better.
improved 表示空气质量正在变好，所以选择 was improving。
Why not the other options? / 为什么其他选项不对？
I. Not all areas were stated to be unhealthy.
II. The condition changed after the downpour.
Reading skill / 阅读技巧： Spot change words: improved, increased, dropped, remained.

Question 5

Source / 原文： Natural Wonders: Niagara Falls, Canada; Great Barrier Reef, Australia; Amazon Rainforest, Ecuador. The Great Barrier Reef is a coral reef in the Pacific Ocean.
Which place would you go to see dolphins and whales?
I. The Niagara Falls, Canada
II. The Great Barrier Reef, Australia
III. The Amazon Rainforest, Ecuador
Answer / 答案： II — The Great Barrier Reef, Australia
Key clue / 关键线索： The supplied answer key marks II.
Why this answer? / 为什么？
The answer key identifies the Great Barrier Reef. Among the three choices, it is the marine environment.
答案表指定 Great Barrier Reef。三个选项中，它属于海洋环境。
Why not the other options? / 为什么其他选项不对？
I. Niagara Falls is a waterfall.
III. The Amazon is a rainforest.
Reading skill / 阅读技巧： Classify each place by environment.
Teacher note / 老师备注： Visible worksheet text does not explicitly mention dolphins and whales, so this teacher version follows the supplied answer key without adding unsupported source detail.

Question 6

Source / 原文： Malacca text: “Strolling along Jalan Hang Jebat and Jalan Tan Cheng Lock, my friends and I could imagine how they would have appeared in ancient times when they were known as Jonker and Herren Street.”
The word they refers to
I. the two streets
II. the writer's friends
III. the last few centuries
Answer / 答案： I — the two streets
Key clue / 关键线索： Jalan Hang Jebat and Jalan Tan Cheng Lock
Why this answer? / 为什么？
The pronoun refers back to the two streets, which had different names in ancient times.
they 指前面的两条街，因为后文说它们以前有其他街名。
Why not the other options? / 为什么其他选项不对？
II. The writer’s friends were not called Jonker and Herren Street.
III. Centuries cannot be known as street names.
Reading skill / 阅读技巧： Pronoun reference: go back to the nearest logical plural noun.

Question 7

Source / 原文： SAMSONG G19 advert: “The value advantage”, “Truly affordable price”, “We accept trade-in and credit cards.”
Which of the following statements is true about the advertisement?
I. The company offers after-sales services.
II. Payment can be made by debit card.
III. It is reasonably priced.
Answer / 答案： III — It is reasonably priced.
Key clue / 关键线索： Truly affordable price
Why this answer? / 为什么？
Affordable means reasonably priced.
affordable 表示价格合理、负担得起，所以 III 正确。
Why not the other options? / 为什么其他选项不对？
I. After-sales service is not mentioned.
II. Credit cards are mentioned, not debit cards.
Reading skill / 阅读技巧： Match synonyms: affordable = reasonably priced.

Question 8

Source / 原文： Time capsule label: “Date Buried” and “Do Not Open Until”.
What are the dates written on a time capsule?
I. The date the time capsule is buried and will be opened.
II. The birth dates of the one sending and receiving.
III. The date the time capsule is prepared and buried.
Answer / 答案： I — The date the time capsule is buried and will be opened.
Key clue / 关键线索： Date Buried + Do Not Open Until
Why this answer? / 为什么？
The two labels show the burial date and the future opening date.
两个标签分别表示埋下日期和未来可开启日期，因此答案是 I。
Why not the other options? / 为什么其他选项不对？
II. No birth dates are requested.
III. No separate preparation date is shown.
Reading skill / 阅读技巧： Use labels in diagrams as direct evidence.
