Question 1

Source / 原文： Nancy writes: “I won’t be able to attend your birthday party. I’ll be going to Sabah with my family.”
Alan will celebrate his birthday
A. in Korea
B. without Nancy
C. once she gets back from Sabah
Answer / 答案： B — without Nancy
Key clue / 关键： I won’t be able to attend your birthday party
Why? / 为什么？
Nancy clearly says she cannot attend, so Alan will celebrate without her.
Nancy 明确说自己不能参加生日会，所以 Alan 会在没有 Nancy 的情况下庆祝。
Why are the others wrong? / 其他选项为什么错？
A: Korea is not mentioned.
C: The party will happen before Nancy returns, not after.
Teacher Focus / 教学重点： Identify what will happen despite one person being absent.

Question 2

Source / 原文： Fly Travel Agency advert: “Discover South Korea with Our Special Travel Package!” 30% off, 10 June–31 August.
The special travel package
A. starts in July
B. takes us to South Korea
C. offers half-price tickets
Answer / 答案： B — takes us to South Korea
Key clue / 关键： Discover South Korea
Why? / 为什么？
The destination is stated directly in the advertisement: South Korea.
广告直接写明目的地是 South Korea，所以答案是 B。
Why are the others wrong? / 其他选项为什么错？
A: The offer starts in June, not July.
C: The discount is 30%, not 50%.
Teacher Focus / 教学重点： Read dates, destination and discount separately.

Question 3

Source / 原文： Kasturis messages Aunt Fatimah: “I’ve received the parcel you sent to me. Thank you very much for the book.”
Aunt Fatimah
A. sent a book to Kasturis
B. read a book to Kasturis
C. received a book from Kasturis
Answer / 答案： A — sent a book to Kasturis
Key clue / 关键： the parcel you sent to me + thank you for the book
Why? / 为什么？
Kasturis thanks Aunt Fatimah for the book she sent, so Aunt Fatimah was the sender.
Kasturis 感谢 Aunt Fatimah 寄来的书，因此 Aunt Fatimah 是寄书的人。
Why are the others wrong? / 其他选项为什么错？
B: No one is reading aloud.
C: The direction is reversed; Kasturis received the book.
Teacher Focus / 教学重点： Track sender and receiver carefully.

Question 4

Source / 原文： Nurul writes about a school picnic. “The fee is only RM50.00. The thing is, only 46 students can join the activity. So, if you are interested, please sign up first thing tomorrow morning.”
Nurul urges Yatin to
A. join her for the picnic
B. get well as soon as possible
C. sign up quickly for the picnic
Answer / 答案： C — sign up quickly for the picnic
Key clue / 关键： only 46 students + sign up first thing tomorrow morning
Why? / 为什么？
Limited places create urgency, so Nurul is urging Yatin to register quickly.
只有 46 个名额，而且要求明天一早就报名，所以重点是尽快报名。
Why are the others wrong? / 其他选项为什么错？
A: Nurul gives information and urges registration, not merely an invitation.
B: She hopes Yatin feels better, but that is not the main urging.
Teacher Focus / 教学重点： Urgency clues: only, hurry, first thing tomorrow.

Question 5

Source / 原文： Kuala Selangor Nature Park poster: “Enjoy your trip but remember, leave nothing but footprints.”
The poster reminds visitors not to
A. destroy the plants
B. drop litter in the park
C. enjoy their trip at the park
Answer / 答案： B — drop litter in the park
Key clue / 关键： leave nothing but footprints
Why? / 为什么？
The phrase means visitors should leave no rubbish or belongings behind.
leave nothing but footprints 的意思是除了脚印什么都不要留下，也就是不要乱丢垃圾。
Why are the others wrong? / 其他选项为什么错？
A: Plant destruction is not directly mentioned.
C: The poster actually tells visitors to enjoy the trip.
Teacher Focus / 教学重点： Interpret environmental slogans from context.

Question 6

Source / 原文： David writes: “Uncle Kefli called just now... Please give him a ring as soon as you arrive home.”
David wrote the message to
A. remind Mother to call Uncle Kefli immediately
B. inform Mother about Uncle Kefli's plan for tomorrow
C. tell Mother that Uncle Kefli had to go to the supermarket
Answer / 答案： A — remind Mother to call Uncle Kefli immediately
Key clue / 关键： Please give him a ring as soon as you arrive home
Why? / 为什么？
The main action David wants Mother to take is to call Uncle Kefli as soon as she gets home.
David 最主要的要求是妈妈一回家就打电话给 Uncle Kefli，因此 A 最准确。
Why are the others wrong? / 其他选项为什么错？
B/C are details from the message, but not the main purpose.
Teacher Focus / 教学重点： Main-purpose questions: focus on the action requested.

Question 7

Source / 原文： Advertisement: “WE do all the WORK, YOU have all the Fun!” It mentions food for children and “Chuckles and Nono, the clowns.”
From the advertisement, we know that the main service offered is
A. cleaning houses
B. looking after children
C. organising children's parties
Answer / 答案： C — organising children's parties
Key clue / 关键： food for children + clowns + fun
Why? / 为什么？
The combination of food, clowns and entertainment shows that the service is for children’s parties.
食物、小丑和娱乐组合明显是在提供儿童派对服务，因此答案是 C。
Why are the others wrong? / 其他选项为什么错？
A: No house cleaning is mentioned.
B: Childcare alone does not explain clowns and party food.
Teacher Focus / 教学重点： Infer service type from multiple clues, not one word.

Question 8

Source / 原文： Tip: “If you slurp hot coffee or tea too fast, and burn your tongue, quickly get a teaspoon of sugar... This will keep your tongue from getting sore.”
This is a tip for someone who
A. often drinks too fast
B. has burnt one’s tongue
C. cannot take too much sugar
Answer / 答案： B — has burnt one’s tongue
Key clue / 关键： and burn your tongue
Why? / 为什么？
The advice is specifically for treating a tongue that has been burnt by a hot drink.
这条建议是针对喝热饮后烫伤舌头的人，所以答案是 B。
Why are the others wrong? / 其他选项为什么错？
A: Drinking too fast is the cause, not the condition the tip is treating.
C: The tip actually tells the person to use sugar.
Teacher Focus / 教学重点： Distinguish cause from target problem.
