Question 1

Source / 原文： Afrida tells Geneva: “Pak Nik will be coming over next week to teach us how to grill satay. He’ll also be teaching us how to make ketupat and satay sauce. I know that you’ve been looking forward to this for a long time.”
Geneva wants to
A. meet Pak Nik
B. learn how to grill satay
C. make ketupat for Afrida
Answer / 答案： B — learn how to grill satay
Key clue / 关键： teach us how to grill satay + looking forward to this
Why? / 为什么？
The message highlights the lesson on grilling satay as something Geneva has been looking forward to.
信息重点是 Pak Nik 会教大家 grill satay，而且 Geneva 已经期待很久，因此答案是 B。
Why are the others wrong? / 其他选项为什么错？
A: Meeting Pak Nik is not identified as her goal.
C: She is learning to make ketupat, not making it specifically for Afrida.
Teacher Focus / 教学重点： Identify the activity the person is looking forward to.

Question 2

Source / 原文： Notice: “The Residents’ Association of Mutiara Apartments will be hosting an open house in celebration of Hari Raya Haji... All the residents are welcome to the event.”
The open house is held
A. for one week
B. for the residents of Mutiara Apartments
C. to celebrate Chinese New Year
Answer / 答案： B — for the residents of Mutiara Apartments
Key clue / 关键： All the residents are welcome
Why? / 为什么？
The event is organised by the Residents’ Association for the residents of Mutiara Apartments.
活动由 Mutiara Apartments 的居民协会举办，并写明所有居民都欢迎参加，所以答案是 B。
Why are the others wrong? / 其他选项为什么错？
A: The notice says the event will be held one week from today, not for one week.
C: It celebrates Hari Raya Haji, not Chinese New Year.
Teacher Focus / 教学重点： Watch prepositions: “one week from today” ≠ “for one week”.

Question 3

Source / 原文： Jack Chan emails Edward: “I found a wallet that may belong to you... There was a note inside the wallet with your email address written. Please do contact me if it is your wallet so that I may return it to you.”
The email is
A. asking Edward to confirm if the wallet belongs to him
B. telling Edward that he had lost his wallet
C. asking Edward to go to the Sri Raya MRT Station
Answer / 答案： A — asking Edward to confirm if the wallet belongs to him
Key clue / 关键： Please do contact me if it is your wallet
Why? / 为什么？
Jack is not certain the wallet is Edward’s, so he asks Edward to confirm ownership.
Jack 只是怀疑钱包可能属于 Edward，因此要求 Edward 确认是不是自己的。
Why are the others wrong? / 其他选项为什么错？
B: Jack does not know for certain that Edward lost it.
C: He does not ask Edward to go to the station.
Teacher Focus / 教学重点： Certainty words matter: may belong ≠ definitely belongs.

Question 4

Source / 原文： Dad writes to Ivy: “Your mother and I have gone out to buy some groceries. You will find your dinner in the microwave oven. Please remember to heat it up.”
Dad wants Ivy to
A. buy some groceries
B. heat up her dinner
C. prepare dinner on her own
Answer / 答案： B — heat up her dinner
Key clue / 关键： Please remember to heat it up
Why? / 为什么？
The dinner is already prepared and in the microwave; Ivy only needs to heat it.
晚餐已经准备好放在微波炉里，Ivy 只需要把它加热。
Why are the others wrong? / 其他选项为什么错？
A: The parents are buying the groceries.
C: Ivy does not need to prepare dinner from scratch.
Teacher Focus / 教学重点： Follow direct instructions rather than background details.

Question 5

Source / 原文： Video Games Workshop: “Stretch your creative muscles and learn how to make your very own video game!”
Those attending the workshop can be expected to be
A. athletic
B. creative
C. charismatic
Answer / 答案： B — creative
Key clue / 关键： Stretch your creative muscles
Why? / 为什么？
The workshop focuses on designing and creating video games, so creativity is the most relevant quality.
工作坊强调制作自己的 video game，并直接提到 creative，因此参加者应具备或发展 creativity。
Why are the others wrong? / 其他选项为什么错？
A: “muscles” is figurative here, not physical fitness.
C: Charisma is unrelated to the workshop focus.
Teacher Focus / 教学重点： Recognise figurative language: creative muscles is not about sport.

Question 6

Source / 原文： Mary emails Ella: “My sister recently won three tickets to Lark Water Park. I was wondering if you would like to join us.”
Mary is
A. telling Ella to go to the water park
B. inviting Ella to go to the water park
C. encouraging Ella to go to the water park
Answer / 答案： B — inviting Ella to go to the water park
Key clue / 关键： would like to join us
Why? / 为什么？
This is a direct invitation asking Ella whether she would like to join them.
“Would you like to join us” 是典型邀请句型，所以答案是 inviting。
Why are the others wrong? / 其他选项为什么错？
A: Mary is not ordering/telling Ella to go.
C: She is offering an invitation, not merely encouragement.
Teacher Focus / 教学重点： Invitation phrase: Would you like to...?

Question 7

Source / 原文： Vacancy advert: “Immediate Vacancies for: Baristas... in-house training on brewing coffee at IOI City Mall.”
Baristas probably work in
A. restaurants
B. hair salons
C. orchards
Answer / 答案： A — restaurants
Key clue / 关键： brewing coffee
Why? / 为什么？
Baristas prepare and serve coffee, so among the options, restaurants are the appropriate workplace.
Barista 的工作与冲泡、供应咖啡有关，因此在这些选项中 restaurants 最合理。
Why are the others wrong? / 其他选项为什么错？
B/C are unrelated to coffee preparation and service.
Teacher Focus / 教学重点： Use occupation vocabulary and workplace association.

Question 8

Source / 原文： Uncle Jom’s Western Food advert: “Father’s Day Special Offer — 30% off ALL main courses. For families dining with their mothers.”
The discount is given
A. when we order main courses
B. in celebration of Father's Day
C. to all customers of the restaurant
Answer / 答案： B — in celebration of Father's Day
Key clue / 关键： Father’s Day Special Offer
Why? / 为什么？
The advertisement explicitly presents the discount as a Father’s Day special offer.
广告标题直接写 Father’s Day Special Offer，因此折扣是为了庆祝 Father’s Day。
Why are the others wrong? / 其他选项为什么错？
A: Main courses are the items discounted, but the reason/purpose of the promotion is Father’s Day.
C: It is not stated to apply to every customer without conditions.
Teacher Focus / 教学重点： Distinguish promotion condition from promotion occasion.
