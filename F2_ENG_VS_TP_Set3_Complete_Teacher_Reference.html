Paper 2 · Part 1 — Writing

Original task: This is a note from your friend, Ava. In about 80 words, reply to your friend to describe your experience in a public speaking competition. Inform her about your experience and add other relevant information.

Suggested Answer / 建议答案
Dear Ava, Thank you for your support. I wanted to share my excitement with you - I won first place in the public speaking competition! It was nerve-wracking, but I managed to deliver my speech confidently and effectively. The topic was challenging, but I did a lot of research and practised numerous times before the competition. The judges complimented me on my well-organised and interesting speech. It was a fantastic experience, and I learned a lot from it. From, Carl (76 words)
Key / 关键： experience + first place + nervousness + preparation + judges’ feedback + what was learned.
Why it works / 为什么： The response directly answers Ava by describing the competition experience and adds relevant supporting details.
范文直接回应 Ava，说明比赛经历，并补充名次、准备过程、评审反馈和收获。

Paper 2 · Part 2 — Writing

Original task: The Nature Club went on a camping trip to Cameron Highlands. In about 80–100 words, write an essay about the camping trip and describe your experience.

Suggested Answer / 建议答案
The Nature Club went on a camping trip to Cameron Highlands on June 25th. Encik Danial and eight students took a bus to the campsite and pitched tents upon arrival. We hiked for two hours in the forest, where Encik Danial pointed out various bird and plant species. We made a campfire and cooked our dinner. We sat around the campfire and told stories once our bellies were filled. The following day, we visited a tea plantation. We also took a lot of photos of the hills, and learned about tea-making. The camping trip was a fantastic opportunity to learn about nature while also relaxing. It was an exciting adventure. (110 words)
Key / 关键： Cameron Highlands + June 25th + Encik Danial + eight students + bus + tents + hike + campfire + tea plantation + learning about nature.
Why it works / 为什么： The essay follows the trip in sequence and describes both activities and the writer’s overall experience.
文章按照行程顺序写出交通、扎营、远足、营火、茶园和整体感受。
