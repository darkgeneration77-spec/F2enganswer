Set 3 — Akeef's Tips for Staying Cool

Teacher’s Reference Version ｜ 英文 + 华语解释

Original Passage / 原文

Akeef's Tips for Staying Cool（保持凉爽的秘诀） The weather（天气） can get extremely hot（非常炎热） at times. It can get so hot that you can feel the heat（热气） in the walls when you enter the room（房间）. Usually, those houses with air conditioners（冷气） will quickly switch them on full blast（全力运作） and stay indoors（在室内） as much as possible to enjoy the cool air. But what can you do if you only have fans（风扇） at home? Usually, Akeef places a pail of water（一桶水） under the fans. ‘I read about it on the Internet（互联网）. It helps to keep the room cool（凉爽）,’ he commented（评论）. ‘Also, I will close most of the windows（窗户）. That way the hot air（热空气） doesn’t enter the house. But at night（晚上）, I open the windows to allow（让） cool air in.’ He also goes around his house to switch off all the unused（未使用的） appliances（电器） and devices（设备） such as computers（电脑） and televisions（电视） as these electrical appliances（电器） release heat（释放热量） even when they are not being used. Akeef always avoids（避免） clothes made from artificial materials（人造材料） such as polyester（聚酯纤维） and nylon（尼龙） even though there's no heatwave（热浪）. He mainly wears clothes made from natural fibres（天然纤维） like cotton（棉） and linen（亚麻） to help him stay cool outdoors（户外）. ‘If I have to walk under the sun（太阳）, I will wear white-coloured clothes（白色衣服）; certainly not black（黑色）!’ he said. Loose clothing（宽松的衣服） also helps him to stay cool. On hotter days, Akeef drinks more water and fresh juice（新鲜果汁） to stay hydrated（保持水分）. ‘I don’t avoid hot drinks（热饮）. As a matter of fact（事实上）, hot drinks make you sweat（流汗） and sweat keeps you cool.’ In addition, he also tries to eat smaller, lighter meals（少量清淡的餐点）. He never has large protein-rich meals（高蛋白餐） that will make his body warm up（变热）. These are the tips（建议） that he always follows. You can try them out too.
