Question 1

Source / 原文： Dino writes: “I’m planning on baking a cake for Xiao Qian’s birthday. I could bake a vanilla cake, a red velvet cake or a cheesecake. What should I bake?”
From the message, we know that Dino is
A. good at baking
B. celebrating his birthday soon
C. excited to buy a cake for Xiao Qian
Answer / 答案： A — good at baking
Key clue / 关键： could bake a vanilla cake / red velvet cake / cheesecake
Why? / 为什么？
Dino is able to consider baking several different types of cake himself, which supports the answer that he is good at baking.
Dino 能自己烘焙多种蛋糕，说明他擅长 baking。题目没有说是 Dino 的生日，也没有说他要买蛋糕。
Why are the others wrong? / 其他选项为什么错？
B: It is Xiao Qian’s birthday, not Dino’s.
C: Dino plans to bake, not buy, the cake.
Teacher Focus / 教学重点： Inference: distinguish what is directly stated from what can reasonably be inferred.

Question 2

Source / 原文： Chao Yi writes from Tibet: “It is very cold as it is snowing heavily here... I had a headache yesterday but I am a lot better after taking some medication.”
The email tells us that Chao Yi
A. does not like snow
B. is enjoying his trip to Tibet
C. was not feeling well yesterday
Answer / 答案： C — was not feeling well yesterday
Key clue / 关键： I had a headache yesterday
Why? / 为什么？
A headache shows Chao Yi was unwell yesterday. The email also says he is better now after medication.
Chao Yi 昨天头痛，说明昨天身体不舒服；吃药后现在已经好多了。
Why are the others wrong? / 其他选项为什么错？
A: He never says he dislikes snow.
B: The email does not explicitly say he is enjoying the trip.
Teacher Focus / 教学重点： Time clue: yesterday points to a past condition.

Question 3

Source / 原文： Nick writes: “I have recently started preparing for my first ever rock climbing trip... advice for someone who is just starting out.”
From the email, we know that Nick is
A. a beginner in rock climbing
B. an expert in rock climbing
C. a regular rock climber
Answer / 答案： A — a beginner in rock climbing
Key clue / 关键： first ever rock climbing trip + just starting out
Why? / 为什么？
Both phrases clearly identify Nick as a beginner.
“first ever” 和 “just starting out” 都直接说明 Nick 是初学者。
Why are the others wrong? / 其他选项为什么错？
B/C contradict the clues that this is his first experience.
Teacher Focus / 教学重点： Look for beginner markers: first ever, just started, new to.

Question 4

Source / 原文： Aaron writes to Lisa: “I hope you can come to my birthday party next Sunday... The party starts at 6.30 p.m.”
The party is organised by
A. Ava
B. Aaron
C. Kevin
Answer / 答案： B — Aaron
Key clue / 关键： sender/signature: Aaron + my birthday party
Why? / 为什么？
Aaron is the one inviting Lisa to his birthday party, so he is the organiser.
Aaron 发出邀请，而且写的是 “my birthday party”，所以组织者是 Aaron。
Why are the others wrong? / 其他选项为什么错？
A/C are not the sender and are not identified as organisers.
Teacher Focus / 教学重点： Check sender and possessive words such as my/our.

Question 5

Source / 原文： John’s Pasta advert: “FREE Drink, Soup and Dessert. For every purchase of a pasta dish during lunch time.”
We can get a free drink at John's Pasta if we
A. come to the shop in the morning
B. buy a bowl of soup
C. buy a pasta dish at noon
Answer / 答案： C — buy a pasta dish at noon
Key clue / 关键： purchase of a pasta dish during lunch time
Why? / 为什么？
Noon falls within lunch time, and the free drink is offered with the purchase of a pasta dish.
中午属于 lunch time，而且优惠条件是购买 pasta dish，所以答案是 C。
Why are the others wrong? / 其他选项为什么错？
A: Morning is not the stated promotion period.
B: Soup is part of the free offer, not the item to purchase.
Teacher Focus / 教学重点： Promotions: match both purchase condition and time condition.

Question 6

Source / 原文： Malaysia Day Expo lists: Traditional Dance Performances, Historical Exhibits, Cookery Demonstration. Entry is free.
During the event, visitors can
A. buy historical exhibits
B. perform traditional dances
C. watch a cookery demonstration
Answer / 答案： C — watch a cookery demonstration
Key clue / 关键： Cookery Demonstration
Why? / 为什么？
The programme lists a cookery demonstration for visitors to watch.
活动项目明确列出 Cookery Demonstration，因此访客可以观看烹饪示范。
Why are the others wrong? / 其他选项为什么错？
A: Historical exhibits are displayed, not stated to be for sale.
B: Traditional dance performances are presented to visitors; visitors are not told to perform.
Teacher Focus / 教学重点： Differentiate audience activity from performer activity.

Question 7

Source / 原文： Siew Fen writes to Professor Frank: “Thank you so much for replying... happy to hear words of encouragement from an actual scientist like you... help me on my path to realising my dreams to be someone just like you.”
According to the email, Siew Fen wants to
A. become a scientist
B. receive a reply from Professor Frank
C. hear words of encouragement from Professor Frank
Answer / 答案： A — become a scientist
Key clue / 关键： dreams to be someone just like you
Why? / 为什么？
Professor Frank is a scientist, and Siew Fen says her dream is to become someone like him.
Professor Frank 是科学家，而 Siew Fen 说她梦想成为像他一样的人，因此她想成为 scientist。
Why are the others wrong? / 其他选项为什么错？
B/C have already happened; she is thanking him for the reply and encouragement.
Teacher Focus / 教学重点： Separate the writer’s long-term goal from things that have already happened.

Question 8

Source / 原文： Ali writes to Chis: “Sorry, I won’t be able to attend the concert with you next week. Could you help get the autograph of the artist for me?”
Ali is
A. asking a favour from Chis
B. signing his autograph for Chis
C. going to attend the concert with Chis
Answer / 答案： A — asking a favour from Chis
Key clue / 关键： Could you help get the autograph ... for me?
Why? / 为什么？
Ali is requesting Chis to do something for him, which is asking a favour.
Ali 请 Chis 帮他拿艺人的签名，这是在请求帮忙，也就是 asking a favour。
Why are the others wrong? / 其他选项为什么错？
B: Ali is not the artist signing an autograph.
C: He explicitly says he cannot attend the concert.
Teacher Focus / 教学重点： Request language: Could you help...? = asking a favour.
