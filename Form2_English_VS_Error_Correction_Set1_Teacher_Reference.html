Original Text / 原文

A local company is scouting and collecting used cooking oil from nearby stalls and shops to use (a) its as the main contribution to create “biodiesel”, an alternative type of diesel. Twenty-two rural schools (b) or most fishing boats have their main source of energy this biodiesel to (c) powered their generators. In order to protect the environment and (d) supporting the efforts of local authorities to reduce waste cooking (e) contaminate in the drainage system, the company aims to (f) promoting alternative sources of fuel. A lot of money is needed to clean and (g) repairing the drains that get damaged by the action of dumping waste cooking oil (h) at drains. About 10,000 litres of waste cooking oil is currently collected by the company every month. Every kg of this oil produced approximately 90% biodiesel and 10% glycerine and methanol.

Corrections + Explanation / 答案与讲解

(a) its → it
After use, we need the object pronoun it, not possessive its.
use 后面需要宾格代词 it；its 表示“它的”。
(b) or → and
The sentence joins two groups that both use biodiesel, so the additive conjunction is and.
这里是连接两个都使用 biodiesel 的群体，应使用 and。
(c) powered → power
to + base verb → to power.
不定式结构：to + 动词原形。
(d) supporting → support
Parallel structure: to protect ... and support ...
平行结构：to protect ... and support ...。
(e) contaminate → contamination
After reduce, the sentence needs a noun naming the pollution/problem.
这里需要名词，contaminate 是动词；contamination 才是“污染”。
(f) promoting → promote
aims to + base verb → aims to promote.
aim to 后面接动词原形。
(g) repairing → repair
Parallel verbs after to: to clean and repair.
平行结构：to clean and repair。
(h) at → into
We dump liquid into a drain because it moves from outside to inside.
液体被倒“进”排水沟，表示进入内部，用 into。

Fast Method / 快速口诀

先看结构，再看词性： to + V1 ｜ and 两边平行 ｜ 冠词/动词后判断名词或动词 ｜ 介词看方向。
Quick scan: infinitive → parallel structure → word class → preposition.
