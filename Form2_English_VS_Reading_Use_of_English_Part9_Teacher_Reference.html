Question 1

Source / 原文： Vincent’s results table: English 55% → 60%; Science 50% → 55%; Bahasa Malaysia 44% → 44%; Maths 44% → 45%.
From the table, we know that
A. Vincent's best subject is English
B. Vincent passed in most subjects
C. Vincent studied at the last minute
Answer / 答案： A — Vincent's best subject is English
Key clue / 关键： English has the highest marks in both exams
Why? / 为什么？
English is Vincent’s highest-scoring subject in the table.
English 在两次考试中都是最高分，因此是他的 best subject。
Why are the others wrong? / 其他选项为什么错？
B depends on a pass mark not stated in the table.
C cannot be inferred from scores alone.
Teacher Focus / 教学重点： Read only what the table directly supports.

Question 2

Source / 原文： Girl says: “Thank you for looking after my puppy. It’s in good hands with you.”
When the girl says, in good hands, she means her friend
A. handled the dog gently
B. took good care of the dog
C. held the dog with both hands
Answer / 答案： B — took good care of the dog
Key clue / 关键： in good hands = being cared for by someone capable
Why? / 为什么？
The idiom means the puppy is safe and well cared for.
in good hands 是习语，表示被可靠的人好好照顾。
Why are the others wrong? / 其他选项为什么错？
A/C interpret “hands” too literally.
Teacher Focus / 教学重点： Idiom meaning, not literal meaning.

Question 3

Source / 原文： News: “Six primary pupils of SK Tujuan... died in a hostel fire... victims, four boys and two girls, were aged 7 to 12.”
Which of the following statements is true?
A. The fire broke out at midnight
B. All the fire victims are in primary school
C. Only ten were in the hostel when the fire broke out
Answer / 答案： B — All the fire victims are in primary school
Key clue / 关键： Six primary pupils
Why? / 为什么？
The report explicitly says all six victims were primary pupils.
报道直接写 six primary pupils，因此所有遇难者都是小学生。
Why are the others wrong? / 其他选项为什么错？
A says around 1 a.m., not midnight.
C gives no total number of hostel occupants.
Teacher Focus / 教学重点： Direct factual statement.

Question 4

Source / 原文： “Zen lives with her protective brother, Ben... Her brother is determined to keep Chris away from her.”
What kind of brother is Ben?
A. selfish
B. caring
C. unkind
Answer / 答案： B — caring
Key clue / 关键： protective brother + determined to keep danger away
Why? / 为什么？
Ben is protective because he cares about Zen’s safety.
Ben 想保护 Zen 远离危险，说明他是 caring。
Why are the others wrong? / 其他选项为什么错？
A/C contradict his protective behaviour.
Teacher Focus / 教学重点： Character trait from actions.

Question 5

Source / 原文： Siti writes: “I am eager to start the new term. Will tell you more about my classes next week.”
How does Siti feel about her classes?
A. excited
B. worried
C. nervous
Answer / 答案： A — excited
Key clue / 关键： eager to start
Why? / 为什么？
Eager shows positive anticipation and excitement.
eager 表示迫不及待、期待，因此情绪是 excited。
Why are the others wrong? / 其他选项为什么错？
B/C are negative feelings not supported.
Teacher Focus / 教学重点： Emotion vocabulary: eager = excited/enthusiastic.

Question 6

Source / 原文： Water filter advert: “Only RM340 (including delivery) Free Installation.”
The advertisement above makes it clear that customer
A. get a free trial
B. must pay for delivery
C. do not have to pay for delivery
Answer / 答案： C — do not have to pay for delivery
Key clue / 关键： including delivery
Why? / 为什么？
The price already includes delivery, so there is no separate delivery charge.
价格已经 including delivery，所以顾客不需要另外付运费。
Why are the others wrong? / 其他选项为什么错？
A not mentioned.
B contradicts “including delivery”.
Teacher Focus / 教学重点： Interpret pricing terms carefully.

Question 7

Source / 原文： Poster: “NO-POLLUTION ENVIRONMENT EXHIBITION”.
The message of the exhibition is likely to be
A. the effects of pollution
B. ways to avoid pollution
C. the importance of recycling
Answer / 答案： B — ways to avoid pollution
Key clue / 关键： NO-POLLUTION ENVIRONMENT
Why? / 为什么？
The title points toward preventing or avoiding pollution rather than only describing its effects.
标题强调 No-Pollution Environment，更偏向如何避免污染。
Why are the others wrong? / 其他选项为什么错？
A/C are narrower/different themes not stated by the poster title.
Teacher Focus / 教学重点： Infer theme from headline.

Question 8

Source / 原文： Jogging text advises staying within limits, increasing mileage gradually, avoiding overexertion, cooling down and stretching.
What is the text about?
A. Advice to follow when jogging
B. How to do stretching exercises
C. Common injuries while jogging
Answer / 答案： A — Advice to follow when jogging
Key clue / 关键： always remember / increase gradually / stretch your muscles
Why? / 为什么？
The whole passage gives several pieces of jogging advice, not just stretching or injuries.
整段给了多项 jogging 建议，不只是拉筋或受伤问题。
Why are the others wrong? / 其他选项为什么错？
B/C cover only one small detail each.
Teacher Focus / 教学重点： Main idea: choose the option covering all supporting points.
