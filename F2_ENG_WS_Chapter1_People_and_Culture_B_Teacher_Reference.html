Part 2 — Error Correction: The Outdoors

Q1
Answer: staying
Key: I enjoy staying
enjoy + V-ing
enjoy 后面接 V-ing。
Q2
Answer: at
Key: at night
Fixed time expression: at night.
固定搭配 at night。
Q3
Answer: is
Key: Watching the night sky is
The gerund phrase is singular.
Watching... 整个动名词短语作单数主语。
Q4
Answer: go
Key: can go zip-lining... and go canoeing
After modal can, use the base verb; parallel verbs should match.
can 后用动词原形，而且并列结构保持一致。
Q5
Answer: latest
Key: the latest activity
The writer means the most recently tried activity.
表示“最近尝试的活动”，用 latest。
Q6
Answer: isn't
Key: If it sounds dangerous, it isn't
The paragraph is in the present tense and explains tubing is not dangerous.
这里是现在时，意思是“听起来危险，其实并不是”。
Q7
Answer: Furthermore
Key: Furthermore, my tube...
The sentence adds another safety feature.
这里继续补充安全措施，所以用 Furthermore。
Q8
Answer: are
Key: There are instructors
Plural subject “instructors” takes are.
instructors 是复数，所以用 are。
