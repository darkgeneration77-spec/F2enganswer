Set 6 — News about incessant rain in Penang

Teacher’s Reference Version ｜ English + 华语 explanation

Original Passage / 原文

GEORGE TOWN: Penangites（槟城人） have been advised（被劝告） to carry their umbrellas（雨伞） when they leave the house. Due to incessant rain（持续降雨） since yesterday (Wednesday, 8 May), the state has been placed on Yellow alert（黄色警报） for 48 hours. The Malaysian Meteorological Department（马来西亚气象局） has issued Perlis, Kedah and Penang a warning（警告）. In addition, all evacuation centres（疏散中心） have been told to equip themselves with necessities（必需品） and make sure that all equipment（设备） is in good working order. According to Penang Fire and Rescue Department（消防与拯救局） (fire operations and safety) officer Supt Nafis Ariff Abdullah, they are ready to face any emergency such as floods（水灾） or landslides（山崩）. He said that 220 firemen（消防员） are on standby at 20 stations in the state. They are also monitoring the flood-prone areas（易淹水地区）, and their trucks and boats are ready to spring into action. Supt Nafis Ariff Abdullah is also of the opinion that the risk will increase during the full-moon period（满月期间） between May 17 and 22 as the tide（潮水） will be higher then. Esther Ng, a 60-year-old resident of Lengkok P. Ramlee, which is one of the low-lying areas（低洼地区） in the state, said she is prepared for the worst after the last major deluge（暴雨） destroyed almost all the furniture（家具） in her house. Therefore this time, if the rain is heavy and slightly longer, she and her family will start moving their cars to higher ground（高地） and their belongings（财物） to a safer place. Meanwhile, Lee Say Hock, the general manager of Penang Hill Corporation (PHC) said there were no landslides（山崩） reported at Penang Hill. He said that there were no major concerns for landslides so far. Staff trimmed a few trees when they fell after strong winds（强风） yesterday but other than that, nothing happened. According to Lee, their team swept the track every morning as early as 5.30am and also monitored all public areas（公共区域）.
