Part 1 — Short Text / Multiple Choice (Q1–8)

Q1. The label makes it very clear that the spray
A. kills all types of insects
B. must be sprayed on insects
C. is poisonous
Answer / 答案： C — is poisonous
Key clue / 关键： label: “Poison!”
Why? / 为什么？
The warning word “Poison!” directly tells us that the spray is poisonous.
标签直接写着 Poison，因此喷雾有毒。
Why not the others? / 其他选项为什么错？
A: It does not say it kills every type of insect.
B: The label does not say it must be sprayed directly on insects.
Teacher Focus / 教学重点： Use explicit warning words.
Q2. The pie chart above shows that more families have
A. one or two children
B. more than two children
C. only one child
Answer / 答案： B — more than two children
Key clue / 关键： 3 children 30% + 4 children 25% + more than 4 children 15% = 70%
Why? / 为什么？
Families with more than two children total 70%, which is the largest group.
超过两个孩子的家庭合计 70%，明显最多。
Why not the others? / 其他选项为什么错？
A: One or two children total only 30%.
C: One child is only 10%.
Teacher Focus / 教学重点： Combine categories before comparing.
Q3. Each customer can buy
A. any amount while stocks last
B. 40 sachets only
C. two packets only
Answer / 答案： C — two packets only
Key clue / 关键： “Only 2 per customer”
Why? / 为什么？
The purchase limit is two packets per customer. The 40 sachets describe the contents of each packet.
每位顾客最多买两包；40 sachets 是每包的数量，不是购买上限。
Why not the others? / 其他选项为什么错？
A: The advert gives a clear limit.
B: 40 sachets refers to one packet's contents.
Teacher Focus / 教学重点： Separate package size from purchase limit.
Q4. What is the purpose of Vivian's letter?
A. To advise Kaven on how to prepare for studying overseas
B. To apply for a student visa
C. To tell Kaven she is studying overseas
Answer / 答案： A — To advise Kaven on how to prepare for studying overseas
Key clue / 关键： “You asked for advice in preparing to study in London”
Why? / 为什么？
Vivian gives practical advice about a student visa and suitable clothing for London.
Vivian 是在提供留学准备建议，包括签证和衣服。
Why not the others? / 其他选项为什么错？
B: Kaven is the person who needs to apply for the visa.
C: Vivian is not announcing that she is studying overseas.
Teacher Focus / 教学重点： Purpose from opening sentence.
Q5. By the phrase looking into, the man means
A. investigating
B. looking for Tom
C. looking at
Answer / 答案： A — investigating
Key clue / 关键： police + murder
Why? / 为什么？
The phrasal verb “look into” means investigate a matter carefully.
look into 在这里表示调查，尤其搭配 police 和 murder。
Why not the others? / 其他选项为什么错？
B: Tom is not part of the statement.
C: “Looking at” is too literal and does not fit the police context.
Teacher Focus / 教学重点： Phrasal verb in context.
Q6. Which of the following is true about the advertisement?
A. Buy one free one promotion is available in the second week
B. The cafe is selling food
C. Buy one free one promotion is available on second day
Answer / 答案： C — Buy one free one promotion is available on second day
Key clue / 关键： promotion for the first week
Why? / 为什么？
Because the promotion runs throughout the first week, it is still available on the second day.
优惠持续整个第一周，所以第二天当然仍然有效。
Why not the others? / 其他选项为什么错？
A: It is for the first week, not the second week.
B: The advert talks about coffee, not food in general.
Teacher Focus / 教学重点： Use time-range logic.
Q7. The advertisement is promoting
A. a mobile phone
B. a camera
C. a special offer
Answer / 答案： A — a mobile phone
Key clue / 关键： NOKI 666 + phone-like device
Why? / 为什么？
The item shown and described is a mobile phone. The offer price is only a sales detail.
广告主要推广的是一部 mobile phone，优惠价只是附带促销信息。
Why not the others? / 其他选项为什么错？
B: It is not presented as a stand-alone camera.
C: A special price exists, but the product itself is the phone.
Teacher Focus / 教学重点： Product vs promotion.
Q8. The notice above should interest
A. students sitting for final exams
B. students sitting for PT3
C. all secondary students
Answer / 答案： B — students sitting for PT3
Key clue / 关键： “How to Score in PT3”
Why? / 为什么？
The workshop is specifically designed for students preparing for PT3.
workshop 专门针对 PT3，因此最相关的是应考 PT3 的学生。
Why not the others? / 其他选项为什么错？

Part 2 — Error Correction (Q9–16)

Q9. Childrens → ______
Answer / 答案： Children
Key clue / 关键： irregular plural
Why? / 为什么？
The plural of child is children, not childrens.
child 的复数是不规则形式 children，不能加 s。
Q10. divided of four groups → divided ______ four groups
Answer / 答案： into
Key clue / 关键： divide into
Why? / 为什么？
The fixed expression is divide into groups.
固定搭配是 divide into groups。
Q11. groups then go → groups then ______
Answer / 答案： went
Key clue / 关键： past tense
Why? / 为什么？
The event is narrated in the past, so went matches the tense.
整段是在叙述过去发生的活动，所以用 went。
Q12. One group were allocated → One group ______ allocated
Answer / 答案： was
Key clue / 关键： subject–verb agreement
Why? / 为什么？
“One group” is singular, so use was.
One group 是单数，因此用 was。
Q13. Group twice → Group ______
Answer / 答案： two
Key clue / 关键： number word
Why? / 为什么？
The group number should be written as two, not the adverb “twice”.
这里是“第二组”的编号，应写 two，不是 twice。
Q14. clean the lane → clean the ______
Answer / 答案： lanes
Key clue / 关键： plural noun
Why? / 为什么？
The answer scheme uses the plural lanes.
答案表指定使用复数 lanes。
Q15. clogging drains → ______ drains
Answer / 答案： clogged
Key clue / 关键： adjective/past participle
Why? / 为什么？
Clogged describes drains that are blocked.
clogged 表示“被堵塞的”，用来形容 drains。
Q16. a good days → a good ______
Answer / 答案： day
Key clue / 关键： a + singular noun
Why? / 为什么？
After the article a, use the singular noun day.
a 后面接单数名词，所以用 day。
