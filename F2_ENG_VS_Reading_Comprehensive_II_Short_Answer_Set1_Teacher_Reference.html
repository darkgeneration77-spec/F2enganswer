READING COMPREHENSIVE II · SHORT ANSWER

Set 1 — Sara's Father

Teacher’s Reference Version ｜ 英文 + 华语解释

Original Passage / 原文

Hard work（努力） is the key to success（成功的关键）. A good example of this is Sara's father. He used to work hard helping his boss run a supermarket（超市）. After he got married（结婚） and had two children（孩子）, he found that it was tough（困难） to make ends meet（维持生计） with the income（收入） that he got. He started selling curry puffs（咖喱角） at 8 a.m. at the railway station（火车站） every day. Sara's parents would wake up at 5 a.m. to make the curry puffs together. After that, Sara's father would go to work at the supermarket at 10 a.m. At first（起初）, Sara's parents could only sell a small amount（数量少） of curry puffs they made. Slowly（渐渐地）, they began selling to regular customers（常客） who bought everything quickly. Eventually（最终）, they started selling more local delicacies（本地美食） and snacks（小吃）. Sara still remembers those early days（早期的日子） where her father returned home looking exhausted（筋疲力尽） at about 11 p.m., but he never used that as an excuse（借口） to not get up early on the next day. One day, a businesswoman（女商人） approached（接近） Sara's parents. She asked if they wanted to sell their stuff at a stall（摊位）. “Your food tastes good. I’m sure you’ll be a hit（大受欢迎的）,” she assured them. Sara’s parents had never thought of expanding（扩展） their business（生意） before that. Since running a stall was a full-time job（全职工作） that required two people to handle（处理）, Sara's father quit（辞职） his job at the supermarket. There was no turning back（无法回头） after that. They worked hard day and night（日以继夜）. Even as a child（即使还是孩子）, Sara used to help out a little（帮忙一点点）. Their hard work paid off（得到回报） as their business prospered（生意兴隆）. They managed to save up enough（存够钱） to open a restaurant（餐厅） in two years. Now, they have 20 workers（员工）, and they can rest while the staff take over（接手工作）. On weekends and school holidays（周末和学校假期）, Sara still helps out. She’s learning the trade（学习行业知识） so that she can take over（继承） one day.
