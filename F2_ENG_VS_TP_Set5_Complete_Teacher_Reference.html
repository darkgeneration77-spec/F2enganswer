Paper 2 · Part 1 — First Aid Workshop

Task: In about 80 words, write a note to your friend telling him/her about a first aid workshop.

Suggested Answer / 建议答案
Dear Lily, Hope you're doing well. There is an upcoming first aid workshop at Taman Bunga Community Hall on 30 October 2020. This workshop aims to equip participants with the skills to give emergency treatment. The workshop will cover topics such as CPR, wound care, and how to respond to medical emergencies. By learning these skills, participants will be able to provide basic emergency treatment when needed, which could potentially save someone's life. If you're interested, you can register now at faworkshop@gmail.com. From, Mei Hui (80 words)
Key / 关键： workshop venue + date + purpose + CPR/wound care/emergencies + registration.
Why it works / 为什么？
The response directly informs the friend about the workshop and includes relevant practical details.
答案直接说明讲习班的地点、日期、目的、内容和报名方式，符合题目要求。

Paper 2 · Part 2 — My First Day at School

Task: In about 80–100 words, write an essay of My First Day at School.

Suggested Answer / 建议答案
My first day of school was an exciting and nerve-wracking experience. My mom came with me and we waited with other parents and their kids in front of the school office. When the teacher arrived, she led us to our separate classrooms, and some kids began to cry as their parents couldn't come with them. I didn't cry since I had already been to kindergarten. During recess, I got to know my new classmates and even managed to cheer up some of the crying boys. It was a great day, and I was happy to see my mom waiting for me at the end of it. (106 words)
Key / 关键： arrival at school + classroom experience + recess + feelings + ending.
Why it works / 为什么？
The essay follows a clear chronological sequence and describes feelings and events from the first day at school.
文章按照时间顺序写第一天上学的经历，并加入感受和事件，结构清楚。
