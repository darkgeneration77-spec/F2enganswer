How to use this page

Read the original sentence, identify the grammatical function of the underlined word, then apply the correction. The explanations below focus on the grammar trigger rather than memorising isolated answers.

先判断划线词在句子中的作用，再改词。不要看到“不顺眼”就乱改，语法毕竟不是抽奖。

(a)
Correction: their

English: Use the possessive determiner their before a noun to show possession.

华语： 名词前表示“他们的”要用 their，不能使用宾格或其他代词形式。

(b)
Correction: are

English: A plural subject requires the plural form are.

华语： 复数主语搭配 are。先找真正的主语，再决定 be verb。

(c)
Correction: to

English: The expression requires the preposition to in this context.

华语： 这是介词搭配题。固定搭配中的介词不能凭中文直译。

(d)
Correction: help

English: After a modal verb, use the base form of the verb.

华语： modal verb 后面用原形：can/may/must/should + V1。

(e)
Correction: an

English: Use an before a singular countable noun beginning with a vowel sound.

华语： 单数可数名词前若接元音音素，使用 an。

(f)
Correction: have

English: The plural subject takes have, not has.

华语： 复数主语 → have；第三人称单数才用 has。

(g)
Correction: carefully

English: An adverb is needed to modify the verb, so use carefully.

华语： 修饰动作要用副词：careful（形容词）→ carefully（副词）。

(h)
Correction: them

English: Use the object pronoun them after a verb or preposition when it functions as an object.

华语： 作宾语要用 them，不是 they / their。

(i)
Correction: better

English: A comparison requires the comparative form better.

华语： 出现比较关系时要用比较级。good → better。

(j)
Correction: because

English: Use because to introduce the reason clause.

华语： 后面是原因句时，用 because + 完整句子。

Quick grammar scan 口诀
① 找主语 → singular / plural
② 找 modal → modal + V1
③ 看名词前 → article / possessive
④ 看动词旁 → adjective 还是 adverb
⑤ 看比较 → comparative
⑥ 看连接关系 → reason / contrast / result
