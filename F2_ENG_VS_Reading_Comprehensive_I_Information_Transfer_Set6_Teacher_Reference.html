Set 6 — Scorching temperatures are coming

Original Text

‘Scorching temperatures are coming’, the news headline reads. We have to be prepared for burning weather in the next few months. The report by the meteorological Department shows that this year may be an El Nino year. There might be less rain. Although there might be outlying rain and thunderstorm in the next three months, but the temperature will still remain up to 37 degrees. Due to the hot weather, forest fires and haze are prospective within a few months. The result of the hot weather causes our water levels in dams to drop. Our health will also be affected by the thick haze and so will the agriculture, aviation and tourism sectors. Along the dry season, the government advises the public to avoid going out and drink more water. There are also a few ways to keep cool. First, do not wear thick clothes. You can also take cold showers to lower down your body temperature and also by using air conditioning or fans. By drinking some fresh coconut water, is also one of the ways to cool down yourself. In order to prevent climate change, we should protect the ecosystem and stop clearing the forests as a long-term solution.
