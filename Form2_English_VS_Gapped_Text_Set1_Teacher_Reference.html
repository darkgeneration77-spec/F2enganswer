Form 2 English VS — Gapped Text Set 1

My Father and I — Teacher’s Reference Version
Answer Key: 1E · 2C · 3G · 4B · 5A · 6H

Original Article / 原文

It was nerve-racking to live with a father who was in the military. Life was difficult for me as a child. 1. ______ Nevertheless, it would take years before my younger self would reach such a realization. It all began when I started kindergarten at the age of six. 2. ______ Perhaps because I was still little and didn't know better, once the school uniform was put on, it came with discipline as well. 3. ______ Discipline was an intricate part of our daily lives. I was taught to get up without an alarm clock. We had to dust the bed, shower and have breakfast. 4. ______ Imagine how hectic my morning would be! I grew up and entered primary school, then secondary school. 5. ______ It was tough, yet I later reaped its reward in college. I was often asked, “How do you do it?” I answered, “I guess it's just my way of life.” 6. ______

Options A–H

A. The military upbringing was still the same.
B. All this should be done in the next 15 minutes after one has awakened.
C. Before that, it was much easier to get away with things.
D. I am so furious and disappointed whenever I remember those times.
E. The upbringing that I went through is actually a blessing when I realized the rewards later on in my life.
F. It was never my choice to lead my life like that.
G. It was tough for me at first.
H. Perhaps growing up with a military dad isn’t that bad after all.

Gap 1

Answer / 答案： E — The upbringing that I went through is actually a blessing when I realized the rewards later on in my life.
Key clue / 关键： realization / Nevertheless
Why? / 为什么？
E gives the positive realization; “Nevertheless” then contrasts it with the writer’s younger self not understanding it yet.
E 先写长大后才明白这种教育是 blessing，后面的 Nevertheless 正好转折到小时候还不理解。
Teacher Focus: 前句 + 后句 → pronoun reference → connector → time sequence → topic continuity.

Gap 2

Answer / 答案： C — Before that, it was much easier to get away with things.
Key clue / 关键： kindergarten / Before that
Why? / 为什么？
C refers to the time before kindergarten.
C 的 Before that 回指上幼儿园之前。
Teacher Focus: 前句 + 后句 → pronoun reference → connector → time sequence → topic continuity.

Gap 3

Answer / 答案： G — It was tough for me at first.
Key clue / 关键： Discipline was an intricate part
Why? / 为什么？
G introduces that the discipline was difficult at first.
G 先说起初很辛苦，后面才展开严格纪律。
Teacher Focus: 前句 + 后句 → pronoun reference → connector → time sequence → topic continuity.

Gap 4

Answer / 答案： B — All this should be done in the next 15 minutes after one has awakened.
Key clue / 关键： 15 minutes / hectic morning
Why? / 为什么？
B explains why the morning was hectic.
B 说明所有事情要在 15 分钟内完成，所以早晨很 hectic。
Teacher Focus: 前句 + 后句 → pronoun reference → connector → time sequence → topic continuity.

Gap 5

Answer / 答案： A — The military upbringing was still the same.
Key clue / 关键： primary school → secondary school
Why? / 为什么？
A shows the military upbringing continued unchanged.
A 表示这种 military upbringing 一直延续。
Teacher Focus: 前句 + 后句 → pronoun reference → connector → time sequence → topic continuity.

Gap 6

Answer / 答案： H — Perhaps growing up with a military dad isn’t that bad after all.
Key clue / 关键： just my way of life / conclusion
Why? / 为什么？
H gives the final reflection.
H 是全文总结，呼应后来得到的好处。
Teacher Focus: 前句 + 后句 → pronoun reference → connector → time sequence → topic continuity.
