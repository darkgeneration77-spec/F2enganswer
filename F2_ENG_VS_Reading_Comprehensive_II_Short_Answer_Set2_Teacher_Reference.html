Set 2 — Going Green

Teacher’s Reference Version ｜ 英文 + 华语解释

Original Passage / 原文

Going Green（环保生活） Mei Ling has been trying to lead a different lifestyle（生活方式） for the past few weeks. This was the result of attending many talks（讲座） at school about being environmentally friendly（环保的）. One speaker（演讲者） said in his talk, “It's ok if you can’t do everything. To start with, just do what you can.” So she decided to take that advice into practice（实践）. When she had her meals at coffee shops or restaurants, she would tell the waiter（服务员） that she did not want a straw（吸管）. Instead, she drank with a metal straw（金属吸管） which she took everywhere with her. Her friends did the same thing, so she didn’t feel awkward（尴尬） about it. Also, she kept a pair of chopsticks（筷子） in her bag. This way, she never had to use any disposable（一次性的） chopsticks or plastic cutlery（塑料餐具） which hawkers（小贩） like to provide. At home, Mei Ling did other things to help Mother Earth（地球）. For example, her mother usually used cling wrap（保鲜膜） to cover the food before putting it in the fridge（冰箱）. Sometimes, her mother wrapped sandwiches in cling wrap for Mei Ling to eat during recess（课间休息） at school. Mei Ling was able to persuade（说服） her mother to stop using cling wrap and now, her family uses containers（容器） to store food（储存食物）. One day, Mei Ling noticed a new shop（商店） in her area（地区）. The shop sold biscuits, nuts, lentils and other foodstuff（食品）. The products were stored in large transparent bins（透明箱子） and not packaged（包装） in plastic. Instead, customers（顾客） were encouraged（被鼓励） to bring their own containers when they shopped（购物） there. Mei Ling was happy with the lifestyle changes（生活方式的改变） she had made and the changes that she saw at the new shop. Feeling motivated（有动力的）, she made a promise（承诺） to herself to continue going green（持续环保）.
