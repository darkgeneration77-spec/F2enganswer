Source / 原题资料： Hanim says she would love to participate in the beach clean-up and will see Ainin there next Sunday.
Q1. What is the purpose of Hanim's message?
A. To thank Ainin for inviting her to the beach
B. To accept Ainin's invitation to clean up the beach
C. To inform Ainin that the beach has a lot of plastic rubbish
Answer / 答案： B — To accept Ainin's invitation to clean up the beach
Key clue / 关键： “I'd love to participate... I'll see you there next Sunday.”
Why? / 为什么？
Hanim is confirming that she will join the clean-up.
Hanim 明确表示愿意参加，并说下星期日见，所以是在接受邀请。
Why not the others? / 其他选项为什么错？
A: She does not thank Ainin.
C: Plastic rubbish is a reason for joining, not the main purpose.
Teacher Focus / 教学重点： Main purpose.

Part 2 — Error Correction: My First Job

Q1. introduced me to he staff → ______ staff
Answer / 答案： his
Key clue / 关键： possessive adjective
Why? / 为什么？
Before the noun staff, use the possessive adjective his.
staff 前需要所有格 his。
Teacher Focus / 教学重点： his + noun.
Q2. his assistant giving me a briefing → assistant ______ me
Answer / 答案： gave
Key clue / 关键： past tense
Why? / 为什么？
The story is in the past, so gave matches the narrative.
整段叙述过去，因此用 gave。
Teacher Focus / 教学重点： Tense consistency.
Q3. began to scheduling appointments → to ______ appointments
Answer / 答案： schedule
Key clue / 关键： to + base verb
Why? / 为什么？
After to, use the base verb schedule.
to 后面接动词原形 schedule。
Teacher Focus / 教学重点： to + V1.
Q4. and answers questions → and ______ questions
Answer / 答案： answered
Key clue / 关键： parallel past tense
Why? / 为什么？
The writer scheduled appointments and answered questions; both verbs should be in the past tense.
scheduled 和 answered 是并列过去动作。
Teacher Focus / 教学重点： Parallel verbs.

Q6. I passing them to one of the nurses → I ______ them

Teacher Focus / 教学重点： Tense consistency.
Q7. By a week → ______ a week
Answer / 答案： After

Why? / 为什么？
The supplied key uses After a week, meaning one week later.
答案表用 After a week，表示一周后。
Teacher Focus / 教学重点： Time connector.
Q8. without many supervision → without ______ supervision
Answer / 答案： much
Key clue / 关键： uncountable noun

“Supervision” is uncountable, so use much.
supervision 是不可数名词，所以用 much。
