Question 1

Source / 原文： Electronic gadgets are in continuous demand; electronic companies are “laughing all the way to the bank”.
The expression laughing all the way to the bank suggests that electronic companies are
A. very dangerous
B. finding it funny
C. making a lot of money
Answer / 答案： C — making a lot of money
Key clue / 关键： continuous demand + idiom: laughing all the way to the bank
Why? / 为什么？
This idiom means making a great deal of money or profiting successfully.
这个习语不是“真的一路笑去银行”，而是表示赚了很多钱、获利丰厚。
Why are the others wrong? / 其他选项为什么错？
A: Nothing says the companies are dangerous.
B: The expression is figurative, not literally about finding something funny.
Teacher Focus / 教学重点： Recognise idioms from context. 习语不能逐字翻译，要结合上下文。

Question 2

Source / 原文： Park notice: “It’s our responsibility to keep our parks clean!” and “Practise a ‘carry in, carry out’ approach.”
The notice above is a reminder to
A. dispose of your trash in bags
B. remove your trash from the park
C. collect and store trash in garbage bags
Answer / 答案： B — remove your trash from the park
Key clue / 关键： carry in, carry out
Why? / 为什么？
The phrase means whatever you bring into the park should be taken out with you, including your rubbish.
carry in, carry out 表示带进公园的东西也要自己带出去，所以垃圾不能留在公园里。
Why are the others wrong? / 其他选项为什么错？
A/C focus on putting rubbish in bags, but the notice specifically stresses taking it out of the park.
Teacher Focus / 教学重点： Notice purpose: identify the action the reader is expected to take.

Question 3

Source / 原文： Sign: “WATCH THIS SPACE”.
The sign above means
A. look out for something exciting here
B. watch out – there's a space here
C. watch out for something in space
Answer / 答案： A — look out for something exciting here
Key clue / 关键： WATCH THIS SPACE is a fixed expression
Why? / 为什么？
The expression tells people to expect an announcement, development or something interesting in this place soon.
这是固定表达，意思是“留意这里，接下来会有新消息/精彩内容”，不是叫你真的看一个空间。
Why are the others wrong? / 其他选项为什么错？
B/C interpret the word “space” literally, which is not the intended idiomatic meaning.
Teacher Focus / 教学重点： Fixed expression recognition.

Question 4

Source / 原文： Christine writes to Carol about extra Accounts classes. Carol had described herself/classes like herself as “hopeless”. Christine says, “I need the classes as much as you do.”
From the email, we know that
A. Christine is good at accounts
B. Carol feels she is weak at Accounts
C. Christine is offering to help Carol in accounts
Answer / 答案： B — Carol feels she is weak at Accounts
Key clue / 关键： Carol's own word: “hopeless”
Why? / 为什么？
Carol's description shows that she lacks confidence in her Accounts ability. Christine also says she needs the extra classes herself.
Carol 用 hopeless 来形容自己这类学生，说明她觉得自己的 Accounts 很弱。Christine 也需要补课，并不是来教 Carol。
Why are the others wrong? / 其他选项为什么错？
A: Christine says she needs the classes too.
C: She is not offering tutoring; both are attending Ms Stella's class.
Teacher Focus / 教学重点： Infer feelings from the writer's wording.

Question 5

Source / 原文： Xin says, “I've got butterflies in my stomach. I think I may even forget my lines.” She is reassured before her turn.
We know from the dialogue that Xin
A. is anxious
B. wants to speak loudly
C. is eager to start her turn
Answer / 答案： A — is anxious
Key clue / 关键： butterflies in my stomach + may forget my lines
Why? / 为什么？
These are signs of nervousness and anxiety before performing or speaking.
butterflies in my stomach 表示紧张不安；担心忘词也进一步说明 Xin 很焦虑。
Why are the others wrong? / 其他选项为什么错？
B: Speaking loudly is advice given to Xin, not what she says she wants.
C: Her words show nervousness rather than eagerness.
Teacher Focus / 教学重点： Use emotional clues and idiomatic expressions.

Question 6

Source / 原文： Report: Malaysian-sponsored students in Sweden and Norway will receive an increase in cost-of-living allowance to keep up with the rising cost of living there.
From the report, we know that Malaysian-sponsored students in Sweden and Norway have been
A. living in poverty
B. working part time for extra money
C. experiencing rising cost of living
Answer / 答案： C — experiencing rising cost of living
Key clue / 关键： keep up with the rising cost of living
Why? / 为什么？
The allowance is increased because living costs have risen. The report does not say the students are poor or working part time.
津贴提高是为了应付 rising cost of living，因此可以判断学生面对生活成本上涨。原文没有说他们贫穷或兼职。
Why are the others wrong? / 其他选项为什么错？
A/B are not stated in the report.
Teacher Focus / 教学重点： Do not add information that the text never gives.

Question 7

Source / 原文： Pie chart: Reasons Why Youth Spend Their Time Idling. Meet friends 22%; Looking for adventure 15%; Others 15%; Window Shop 28%; Relax 20%.
About one-fifth of youth who spend their time idling want to
A. meet friends
B. relax
C. shop
Answer / 答案： B — relax
Key clue / 关键： one-fifth = 1/5 = 20%
Why? / 为什么？
The chart shows Relax at exactly 20%. One-fifth is 20%, so B matches.
五分之一 = 20%。图表中 Relax 正好是 20%，所以答案是 B。
Why are the others wrong? / 其他选项为什么错？
A: Meet friends = 22%.
C: Window shop = 28%.
Teacher Focus / 教学重点： Convert fractions to percentages before reading a chart.

Question 8

Source / 原文： Water notice: “Every Drop Counts!” “Dripping faucets and leaky toilets can waste about 4000 gallons...” “STOP THE DROP! Repair all leaks...”
According to the extract, we have to
A. fix all leaking taps
B. conserve water by using less of it
C. repair the water tank
Answer / 答案： A — fix all leaking taps
Key clue / 关键： STOP THE DROP! Repair all leaks
Why? / 为什么？
The extract gives a direct instruction to repair leaks, especially dripping faucets and leaky toilets.
原文直接要求 Repair all leaks，并特别提到 dripping faucets，所以答案是修理漏水的水龙头。
Why are the others wrong? / 其他选项为什么错？
B is a broader idea, but the direct action stated is repairing leaks.
C: No water tank is mentioned.
Teacher Focus / 教学重点： When one option repeats a direct instruction from the text, prefer it over a broad inference.
