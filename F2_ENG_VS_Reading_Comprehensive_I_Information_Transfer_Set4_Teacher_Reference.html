Set 4 — Kana

Teacher’s Reference Version ｜ 英文 + 华语解释

Original Passage / 原文

Kana is a 49 years old man. He lived in a small town known as Puchong, Selangor. He spent most of his time at his grandparents' home because his father worked as a hospital attendant and his mother was a clerk. He spent his time playing with his cousins and neighbours. They enjoyed most of their time playing football in the small field near their house. Sometimes the elder neighbours would join them too. His grandpa liked fishing during free time and will bring them along. Those were the memorable moments.

His grandmother liked to cook. She always cooked him chicken curry and nasi lemak. Those were his favourite dishes. He still remembered how he messed up one time.

When he was in his secondary school, he joined scout for their co-curricular. He used to enjoy hiking and camping. Besides scout, he also joined their school football team and athletics. He was very popular in school. Now, he has become a very successful businessman, but he still remembers those days when he was a kid.
