Form 2 English VS — Gapped Text Set 8

A Replacement for Plastic Bottles — Teacher’s Reference Version
Answer Key: 1G · 2F · 3D · 4H · 5E · 6C

Original Article / 原文

Plastic waste is common at outdoor events, especially marathons. Do you know what a marathon is? 1. ______ Marathon runners cover 42 kilometres. Many water bottles are used. 2. ______ Organisers distributed seaweed pods containing water instead. The pod is better than plastic in many ways. Let's look at cost. 3. ______ It also biodegrades in six weeks. 4. ______ Another special fact: 5. ______ Runners can pop them straight into their mouths, producing no trash. The pods can be used at sports and other large-scale events. 6. ______ Such events usually generate much plastic waste.

Options A–H

A. Everyone was looking around for water to drink.
B. Seaweed could be found everywhere on the roads.
C. For instance, at concerts or road shows.
D. It is cheaper to make a pod than a plastic bottle.
E. They are safe to be eaten.
F. This year, the organisers did something different.
G. It is a long-distance race.
H. Plastic takes hundreds of years to decompose.

Gap 1

Answer / 答案： G — It is a long-distance race.
Key clue / 关键： What is a marathon?
Why? / 为什么？
G directly defines marathon.
题目先问 marathon 是什么，G 就是定义。
Teacher Focus: 前句 + 后句 → pronoun reference → connector → time sequence → topic continuity.

Key clue / 关键： many plastic bottles
Why? / 为什么？
F introduces the organisers’ new solution.
F 先说今年做了不同尝试，后面才说明 seaweed pods。
Teacher Focus: 前句 + 后句 → pronoun reference → connector → time sequence → topic continuity.

Answer / 答案： D — It is cheaper to make a pod than a plastic bottle.
Key clue / 关键： cost
Why? / 为什么？

前句明确说 cost，因此 D 完全对应。
