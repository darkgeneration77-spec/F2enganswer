Set 2 — Planning to save money to buy some birthday present for family

Teacher’s Reference Version ｜ 英文 + 华语解释

Original Passage / 原文

This year, I am planning to save money and buy some birthday presents for my family. Since I am not working, I cannot afford something that is expensive. So, I will get each of them something that is meaningful. I will prepare a cake for my mother. The cost is cheap, it is just RM12. I will get my father a cap for him because he likes to jog in the park in the early morning. The price is just around RM20. For my grandmother, I will get her a light shawl. It is sold near my house, and I saw some of it with beautiful patterns on it, is just RM15. My younger brother loves watching throwing disc video every day. So, I will buy him a throwing disc. I will buy it online and is around RM18. For my sister, I will get her colour pencils and a sketch pad. These gifts will be RM18. The total savings I need is about RM100. With this amount of money, I’ll be able to play around with.
