Form 2 English VS — Gapped Text Set 4

Examination Phobia — Teacher’s Reference Version
Answer Key: 1C · 2E · 3B · 4G · 5H · 6D

Original Article / 原文

Examination phobia refers to excessive worry about upcoming exams. 1. ______ A little nervousness can be healthy, but excessive stress can hamper performance. 2. ______ 3. ______ Some major factors are nervousness, poor preparation, fear of parents’ and teachers’ expectations and competition. Parents and teachers should help students cope. 4. ______ Instead, they should deal with their children lovingly. One way is to formulate a study plan. 5. ______ Also, avoid last-minute cramming. Exercise helps keep the mind calm. Meditation can also reduce anxiety. 6. ______

Options A–H

A. A lot of preparation and expectation are facts that trigger stress among the students.
B. Examination phobia is caused by different factors, which can be personal, parental and teacher-related.
C. It is extremely disastrous as it affects the students psychologically, cognitively and emotionally.
D. Make a habit to meditate at least 10 minutes per day to increase your concentration power.
E. Examination phobia can be a serious barrier to good performance.
F. Lack of predictability and control are sure to make most students feel anxious.
G. Parents should not try to force their own dreams onto their children.
H. Start planning it and executing it well before the exams begin.

Gap 1

Answer / 答案： C — It is extremely disastrous as it affects the students psychologically, cognitively and emotionally.
Key clue / 关键： definition → effects
Why? / 为什么？
C adds the harmful effects after the definition.
定义之后说明严重影响，C 最合适。
Teacher Focus: 前句 + 后句 → pronoun reference → connector → time sequence → topic continuity.

Gap 2

Answer / 答案： E — Examination phobia can be a serious barrier to good performance.
Key clue / 关键： hampers performance
Why? / 为什么？
E paraphrases the effect on performance.
前文说妨碍表现，E 用 barrier to good performance 同义总结。
Teacher Focus: 前句 + 后句 → pronoun reference → connector → time sequence → topic continuity.

Gap 3

Answer / 答案： B — Examination phobia is caused by different factors, which can be personal, parental and teacher-related.
Key clue / 关键： followed by causes
Why? / 为什么？
B introduces the causes before the list.
后面马上列原因，所以前面要总起句 B。
Teacher Focus: 前句 + 后句 → pronoun reference → connector → time sequence → topic continuity.

Gap 4

Answer / 答案： G — Parents should not try to force their own dreams onto their children.
Key clue / 关键： Parents and teachers / Instead
Why? / 为什么？
G sets up the contrast with “Instead”.
G 先说父母不应强迫，后面 Instead 再说应该怎么做。
Teacher Focus: 前句 + 后句 → pronoun reference → connector → time sequence → topic continuity.

Gap 5

Answer / 答案： H — Start planning it and executing it well before the exams begin.
Key clue / 关键： study plan / avoid cramming
Why? / 为什么？
H tells students to plan early.
H 强调提早规划，与 avoid last-minute cramming 呼应。
Teacher Focus: 前句 + 后句 → pronoun reference → connector → time sequence → topic continuity.

Gap 6

Answer / 答案： D — Make a habit to meditate at least 10 minutes per day to increase your concentration power.
Key clue / 关键： meditation
Why? / 为什么？
D gives a practical meditation routine.
前文谈 meditation，D 直接给做法。
Teacher Focus: 前句 + 后句 → pronoun reference → connector → time sequence → topic continuity.
