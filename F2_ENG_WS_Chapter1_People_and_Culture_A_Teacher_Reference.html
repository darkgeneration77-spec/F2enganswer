Source / 原题资料： Selva thanks Uncle Arul for the music books and says he can practise the nice songs on his piano.
Q1. Selva wants his uncle to know
A. that he is really thankful for his gift
B. that he will be using the books in the evening
C. that he wants to learn to play a musical instrument
Answer / 答案： A — that he is really thankful for his gift
Key clue / 关键： “I want to thank you for the music books”
Why? / 为什么？
The main purpose of Selva’s email is to express thanks for the gift. The piano and evening trip are supporting details.
Selva 电邮最主要的目的就是感谢 Uncle Arul 送的 music books。钢琴和晚上去音乐店只是附加资料。
Why not the others? / 其他选项为什么错？
B: Selva does not say he will use the books in the evening.
C: He already plays the piano; the email is not asking to learn an instrument.
Teacher Focus / 教学重点： Main-purpose questions: look at the opening and subject line.

B. meet every afternoon
C. get to take part in a major competition
Answer / 答案： C — get to take part in a major competition
Key clue / 关键： National Squash Tournament
Why? / 为什么？
A national tournament is a major competition, and the notice says members will be trained for it.
National Squash Tournament 属于大型比赛，notice 说明会员会为这个比赛接受训练。
Why not the others? / 其他选项为什么错？
A: The club meets once a week, on Thursday.

Part 2 — Error Correction: My Sister’s Wedding

Passage focus / 文章主题： The narrator describes the preparations for her older sister’s wedding and the wedding day.
Q1. After five years for dating → five years ______ dating
Answer / 答案： of
Key clue / 关键： fixed phrase: years of + noun/V-ing
Why? / 为什么？
The natural structure is five years of dating.
固定结构是 five years of dating，表示“交往了五年”。
Teacher Focus / 教学重点： Collocation: period of time + of + activity.
Q2. My sister but her fiancé wanted → My sister ______ her fiancé wanted
Answer / 答案： and
Key clue / 关键： addition, not contrast
Why? / 为什么？
Both the sister and her fiancé wanted to get married in church, so the ideas are joined with and.
姐姐和未婚夫都想在教堂结婚，是并列关系，所以用 and。
Teacher Focus / 教学重点： and = addition; but = contrast.
Q3. managed to booked → managed to ______
Answer / 答案： book
Key clue / 关键： to + base verb
Why? / 为什么？
After to, use the base form book.
to 后面接动词原形，因此 booked 改为 book。
Teacher Focus / 教学重点： to + V1.
Q4. looked for an venue → looked for ______ venue
Answer / 答案： a
Key clue / 关键： a/an rule
Why? / 为什么？
“Venue” begins with a consonant sound /v/, so use a.
venue 以辅音音 /v/ 开头，所以用 a venue。
Teacher Focus / 教学重点： Use sound, not spelling alone, for a/an.
Q5. a hall that were not too far → a hall that ______ not too far
Answer / 答案： was
Key clue / 关键： subject–verb agreement
Why? / 为什么？
The relative pronoun “that” refers to the singular noun hall, so use was.
that 指前面的单数 hall，因此用 was。
Teacher Focus / 教学重点： Find the noun the relative pronoun refers to.
Q6. hers wedding dress → ______ wedding dress
Answer / 答案： her
Key clue / 关键： possessive adjective before noun
Why? / 为什么？
Before the noun “wedding dress”, use the possessive adjective her.
wedding dress 前面要用形容词性物主代词 her。
Teacher Focus / 教学重点： her + noun; hers stands alone.
Q7. Furthermore, her fiancé shopped... → ______, her fiancé...
Answer / 答案： Meanwhile
Key clue / 关键： time relationship
Why? / 为什么？
Meanwhile means “at the same time” and fits the parallel wedding preparations.
Meanwhile 表示“与此同时”，正好连接姐姐买婚纱和未婚夫买礼服这两件同时进行的事情。
Teacher Focus / 教学重点： Choose connectors by relationship, not just position.
Q8. The wedding had gone smooth → had gone ______
Answer / 答案： smoothly
Key clue / 关键： adverb modifies verb
Why? / 为什么？
An adverb is needed to describe how the wedding went, so use smoothly.
修饰 went/gone 这个动作要用副词 smoothly。
Teacher Focus / 教学重点： Adjective smooth → adverb smoothly.
