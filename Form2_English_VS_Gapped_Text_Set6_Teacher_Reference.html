Form 2 English VS — Gapped Text Set 6

Camouflage among Animals — Teacher’s Reference Version
Answer Key: 1F · 2A · 3H · 4C · 5D · 6G

Original Article / 原文

Some animals hide to protect themselves or hunt prey. 1. ______ The zebra's stripes make its body appear dull grey from afar. 2. ______ Animals with spots can hardly be seen among bushes and trees. Tigers conceal themselves with stripes. Cheetahs must hide. 3. ______ 4. ______ Leaf insects and stick insects are hard to see among leaves and branches. Butterflies blend with flowers. Crocodiles look like floating logs. 5. ______ Chameleons and certain fish change colour in new surroundings. 6. ______ Each species has its own special way of surviving.

Options A–H

A. This camouflages them well, especially when the light is dim at dawn and dusk when most predators are hunting.
B. The giraffes' long necks help them to reach leaves high up in the trees.
C. Some animals, especially insects, are shaped and coloured like their surroundings.
D. Some animals can quickly change colour.
E. The snake is known for its venomous bite.
F. One way of hiding themselves is to use camouflage.
G. Animals are masters of camouflage.
H. Their spotted tawny colour blends with the brown grasslands where they hunt.

Gap 1

Answer / 答案： F — One way of hiding themselves is to use camouflage.
Key clue / 关键： hide → camouflage
Why? / 为什么？
F introduces camouflage before examples.
F 是总起句，后面 zebra 是 camouflage 例子。
Teacher Focus: 前句 + 后句 → pronoun reference → connector → time sequence → topic continuity.
