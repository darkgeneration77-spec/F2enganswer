Set 10 — Jungle trekking

Teacher’s Reference Version ｜ 英文 + 华语解释

Original Passage / 原文

Jungle trekking is generally a very thrilling and adventurous experience. There are a few tips that you need to know before you go jungle trekking.

First, when you are lost, make sure you stay away from wild animals. The way to prevent it is to keep your food inside your bag and not outside. The wild animals will follow the smell of the food and you are going to be in trouble.

Secondly, you can make a fire to signal for help. There is a way to start up the fire. You have to make a small pile of flammable materials. In example, dry leaves and paper can provide combustion. Form a little pyramid of dry sticks above the pile. Light it and you get a fire.

If the surroundings have branches from trees, you can try to build a shelter. You can also find your way out of the jungle by using the compass. If you do not have a compass, you can follow the direction of the sun. The sun rises from the east and sets in the west. If it is noon, it will lead you south.
