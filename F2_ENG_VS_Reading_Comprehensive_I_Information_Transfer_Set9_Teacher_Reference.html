Original Passage / 原文

Mohammad Nur Khalid, commonly known as Lat, is a well-known cartoonist in Malaysia who is gaining worldwide fame. He is also one of the most-read cartoonists in Southeast Asia. When he was young, he drew his comic by translating what he saw, felt and imagined on a big piece of paper. His teacher always took his works and pasted them on the wall.

When Lat was 9 years old, he sold his pencil-drawn comics to his classmates. Although it was just 20 cents, but Lat felt proud. “TigaSekawan”, was published when Lat was in his 6th primary school year. It is Lat’s first real book and the story talked about three heroic friends who team up together to catch robbers.

Lat’s annual compilations were strips printed into book and become very successful with “Kampung Boy”. His books sold more than 100,000 copies. His comics are suitable for children, intellectuals and politicians.

After that, he continued to publish “Town Boy” (1980), Mat Som (1989), Kampung Boy: Yesterday & Today (1993), “Lat 30 Years Later” (1994). These are some of the successful stories that he published. He has already been very successful and made our nation proud of it.
