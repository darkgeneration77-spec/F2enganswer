Source / 原题资料： Danny thanks Siti for a book on healthy eating.
Q1. Danny wants his friend to know
A. that he likes to be eating healthily
B. that he really appreciates the book
C. that he will be sharing the book's recipes
Answer / 答案： B — that he really appreciates the book
Key clue / 关键： “Thank you so much for the book...”
Why? / 为什么？
The main purpose is to thank Siti for the book.
电邮主要目的就是感谢 Siti 送的 healthy eating book。
Why not the others? / 其他选项为什么错？
A: He wants a healthy diet, but that is not the main purpose.
C: He never says he will share the recipes.
Teacher Focus / 教学重点： Main-purpose question.

Part 2 — Error Correction: Losing Weight Healthily

Q1. did not like a way she looked → did not like ______ way
Answer / 答案： the
Key clue / 关键： fixed expression
Why? / 为什么？
The correct phrase is the way she looked.
固定表达是 the way she looked。
Teacher Focus / 教学重点： Article usage.
Q2. Rita's doctor have → doctor ______
Answer / 答案： had
Key clue / 关键： past tense
Why? / 为什么？
The whole story is in the past, so use had.
整段叙述过去，因此用 had。
Teacher Focus / 教学重点： Tense consistency.
Q3. sugary drinks but artificial juices → sugary drinks ______ artificial juices
Answer / 答案： and
Key clue / 关键： addition
Why? / 为什么？
Both sugary drinks and artificial juices are items to avoid, so use and.
两种饮料都是需要避免的对象，因此用 and。
Teacher Focus / 教学重点： and vs but.
Q4. For each meal, she would make... → she ______ make
Answer / 答案： should
Key clue / 关键： advice/modal
Why? / 为什么？
The doctor is giving advice, so should fits the instruction.
医生在给建议，所以用 should。
Teacher Focus / 教学重点： Advice language.
Q5. too many sugary and oily food → too ______
Answer / 答案： much
Key clue / 关键： uncountable noun
Why? / 为什么？
“Food” is uncountable here, so use much.
food 在这里是不可数名词，所以用 much。
Teacher Focus / 教学重点： much + uncountable noun.
Q6. start by exercise → by ______
Answer / 答案： exercising
Key clue / 关键： by + V-ing
Why? / 为什么？
After by, use the -ing form.
by 后面接 V-ing，所以用 exercising。
Teacher Focus / 教学重点： by + V-ing.
Q7. an exercise that she enjoying → she ______
Answer / 答案： enjoyed
Key clue / 关键： past tense
Why? / 为什么？
The sentence refers to what she liked in the past, matching the narrative tense.
整段过去时，所以用 enjoyed。
Teacher Focus / 教学重点： Tense consistency.
Q8. After a few months her began → ______ began
Answer / 答案： she
Key clue / 关键： subject pronoun
Why? / 为什么？
A subject pronoun is needed: she.
这里需要主格代词 she。
Teacher Focus / 教学重点： Pronoun form.
