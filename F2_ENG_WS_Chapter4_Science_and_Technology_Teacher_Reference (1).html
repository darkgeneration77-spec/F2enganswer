Source / 原题资料： Azrul thanks his older sister for helping set up his new computer and printer so he could finish his science project.
Q1. Azrul wants his older sister to know
A. their father is busy with his work
B. that he has a science project to complete
C. that he is grateful to her for setting up the computer
Answer / 答案： C — that he is grateful to her for setting up the computer
Key clue / 关键： “Thank you so much... I really appreciate it.”
Why? / 为什么？
The message is mainly a thank-you note to his sister for helping him set up the equipment.
这封邮件的主要目的就是感谢姐姐帮他安装 computer 和 printer。
Why not the others? / 其他选项为什么错？
A: Dad being busy is background information.
B: The project explains why the help mattered, but it is not the main purpose.
Teacher Focus / 教学重点： Main-purpose question.
Source / 原题资料： Science Club meets every Tuesday and Friday and members can take part in the Invention Convention in November.
Q2. The Science Club members will
A. meet once a week
B. have their meetings in the morning
C. get the chance to take part in the Invention Competition
Answer / 答案： C — get the chance to take part in the Invention Competition
Key clue / 关键： “opportunity to take part in the Invention Convention”
Why? / 为什么？
The club explicitly gives members an opportunity to join an invention event.
club 明确给会员机会参加 invention event，因此 C。
Why not the others? / 其他选项为什么错？
A: They meet twice a week.
B: Meetings are 2.30–4.30 p.m., not morning.
Teacher Focus / 教学重点： Paraphrase opportunity/chance.

Part 2 — Error Correction: Visiting an Orang-Utan Sanctuary

Answers: 1 went · 2 probably · 3 so · 4 us · 5 off · 6 had · 7 clinging · 8 an

Q1. my family and I goes → ______
Answer / 答案： went
Key clue / 关键： past tense
Why? / 为什么？
The school-holiday trip happened in the past, so use went.
假期旅行发生在过去，所以用 went。
Teacher Focus / 教学重点： Past narrative.
Q2. most probable → most ______
Answer / 答案： probably
Key clue / 关键： adverb form
Why? / 为什么？
An adverb is required before the clause “because there was a huge crowd”, so the answer scheme uses probably.
这里需要副词 probably。
Teacher Focus / 教学重点： Word form.
Q3. determined to see the orang-utans or my father... → ______
Answer / 答案： so
Key clue / 关键： cause and result
Why? / 为什么？
They were determined to see the orang-utans, so the father spoke to the ranger.
他们很想看到 orang-utan，所以父亲去找 ranger，属于结果关系。
Teacher Focus / 教学重点： so = result.
Q4. take we to a part of the park → ______
Answer / 答案： us
Key clue / 关键： object pronoun
Why? / 为什么？
After the verb take, use the object pronoun us.
take 后面要用宾格 us。
Teacher Focus / 教学重点： we = subject; us = object.
Q5. had bitten of a couple of fingers → bitten ______
Answer / 答案： off
Key clue / 关键： phrasal verb
Why? / 为什么？
The phrase is bite off, meaning remove by biting.
bite off 表示“咬掉”。
Teacher Focus / 教学重点： Phrasal verb.
Q6. we still has not seen → we still ______ not seen
Answer / 答案： had
Key clue / 关键： past perfect
Why? / 为什么？
The answer scheme uses had not seen to describe the situation before Hot Mamma appeared.
答案表使用 had not seen，表示在 Hot Mamma 出现前仍未看到 orang-utan。
Teacher Focus / 教学重点： Past perfect.
Q7. a baby was cling to her → was ______
Answer / 答案： clinging
Key clue / 关键： past continuous
Why? / 为什么？
After was, use the -ing form: was clinging.
was 后接 V-ing，所以用 clinging。
Teacher Focus / 教学重点： be + V-ing.
Q8. indeed the experience → indeed ______ experience
Answer / 答案： an
Key clue / 关键： article
Why? / 为什么？
“Experience” is singular and begins with a vowel sound, so use an.
experience 是单数且以元音音开头，所以用 an。
Teacher Focus / 教学重点： a/an by sound.
