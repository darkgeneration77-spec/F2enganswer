Set 10 — Letter for Aishah

Teacher’s Reference Version ｜ English + 华语 explanation

Original Passage / 原文

Dear Aishah, Thank you for the card. I have pinned it on my personal noticeboard at home. How's everyone in your family? I hope everyone had a good rest after the holiday. I’m flattered that you wrote to seek my advice on how to help your friend, Monica, overcome her shyness. Well, all of us have experienced shyness at some points in our lives. However, it shouldn't make us feel lonely or affect our self-esteem in any way. Aishah, this is what you can do to help Monica. First, empathise with her situation. Tell her that you know how she feels and that it is alright to feel anxious about speaking in a group. Also, allow her to tell you how she feels. You can also tell her about your own bouts of shyness. Then, you can invite Monica to outings with your circle of friends. Let her interact with different people and she will grow more comfortable being around people if she continues to participate in social interactions. She will not feel alone because you are there to give her support. It would also be a good thing if you could tell her to stop thinking of herself as a shy person. This is because she will continue to act that way to fit the label if she keeps thinking that she is shy. Instead, ask her to think about how and what she wants to be – a confident person. You can be a role model to her as well. She will observe how you initiate a conversation and interact with your friends. She can model that behaviour to help her overcome her shyness. I hope these tips will help you bring your friend out of her shell. Please let me know how everything turns out. I wish you the best of luck. Your cousin, Irfan
