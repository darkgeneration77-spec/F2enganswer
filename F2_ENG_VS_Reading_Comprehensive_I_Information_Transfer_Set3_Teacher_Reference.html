Set 3 — 'Plant a Tree' Campaign by Nature Club

Teacher’s Reference Version ｜ 英文 + 华语解释

Original Passage / 原文

'Plant a Tree' campaign organised by Nature Club. This project is a very interesting project to beautify the school and create an awareness of the environment. Every class will be encouraged to plant a tree and care for it. All the trees must be labelled with their names, the classes and the day that was planted. Day by day, the tree will show the result and it will naturally enhance the environment and provide a shady place.

The trees can be obtained by using class funds or get someone to donate. The type of tree is important. Choose trees that will grow big and provide shady places. Although the surroundings will be dirty with fallen leaves but when the students graduate from school, they will recall the memories related to the trees.

Let's do our part for greener environment by joining the 'Plant a Tree' campaign.
