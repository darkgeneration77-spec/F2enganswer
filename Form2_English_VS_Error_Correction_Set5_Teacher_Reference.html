Original Text + Corrections

A coach is the main reason that produces a good athlete. The goals that are set by the coach can help you to remain focus focused until you achieve it, revealing your weakness and work in on improving your performance. Once you are involved in the a sport, you should be passionate about it. Then, you will obtain achievements easy easily if you keep those long hours of training and stay loyal. Stay positive and thinks think about the success you want to achieve. Always sets set short and long-term goals which you think is capable to achieve. The moment you achieve all the goals you set, you will feel continuous improves improvement. While you are training, make sure you drinks drink enough water before, during and after. You are responsible to take care of yours your body. Make sure you have properly proper meals to restore the energy after your training.

Answers & Explanations

(a) focus → focused
After remain, an adjective is required to describe the athlete's state.
remain 后面这里需要形容词，表示“保持专注”，所以用 focused。
(b) in → on
The fixed expression is work on + noun/gerund.
固定搭配：work on improving = 努力改善。
(c) the → a
The sentence refers to being involved in a sport generally, not one previously identified.
这里第一次泛指“一项运动”，使用 a。
(d) easy → easily
An adverb modifies the verb phrase obtain achievements.
修饰动作 obtain，需要副词 easily。
(e) thinks → think
It is an imperative sentence: Stay ... and think .... Use the base verb.
祈使句动词用原形：Stay ... and think ...。
(f) sets → set
After the implied subject you in an imperative, use the base form set.
Always set ... 是祈使句，用动词原形。
(g) improves → improvement
After the adjective continuous, a noun is needed.
continuous 是形容词，后面要接名词 improvement。
(h) drinks → drink
After you, the present-tense verb is drink, not drinks.
主语 you 配动词原形 drink。
(i) yours → your
A possessive adjective is required before the noun body.
body 是名词，前面要用形容词性物主代词 your，不是 yours。
(j) properly → proper
An adjective is needed to modify the noun meals.
修饰名词 meals，要用形容词 proper。

Grammar Triggers · 快速口诀

remain + adjective · work on + -ing · 动词看主语 · 动词要副词修饰 · 形容词后找名词 · 名词前看形容词 · your + noun，但 yours 后面不能直接再接名词。
