Q1. The purpose of the advertisement is to
A. appeal to people to visit Cannon Bookstore
B. appeal to people to join the Book Club
C. ask people to pay the annual membership fee
Answer / 答案： B — appeal to people to join the Book Club
Key clue / 关键： “We would like to invite you to join us...”
Why? / 为什么？
The advertisement’s main purpose is recruitment into the George Town Book Club. The venue and membership fee are supporting details.
广告最主要的目的，是邀请人们加入 George Town Book Club；地点和会费只是附加资料。
Why not the others? / 其他选项为什么错？
A. Cannon Bookstore is only the venue.
C. The RM7 fee is information, not the main purpose.
Teacher Focus / 教学重点： Purpose question: choose the option covering the whole advertisement.

Q9. to living → to ______
Correct answer / 正确答案： live
Key Grammar / 关键语法： to + base verb
Why? / 为什么？
After to, use the base form live.
to 后面接动词原形，所以 living 改成 live。
Q10. lots of complain → lots of ______
Correct answer / 正确答案： complaints
Key Grammar / 关键语法： lots of + plural count noun
Why? / 为什么？
“Complaint” is countable here, so after “lots of” use the plural noun complaints.
lots of 后面这里接可数名词复数，因此用 complaints。

Teacher note / 老师备注： The surrounding sentence begins “She said that...”, so another tense could be argued in free editing. This teacher version preserves the supplied answer scheme exactly.
Q12. may loose her job → may ______ her job
Correct answer / 正确答案： lose
Key Grammar / 关键语法： modal + base verb; lose vs loose
Why? / 为什么？
After may, use the base verb lose. “Loose” is an adjective/verb with a different meaning.
may 后面接动词原形 lose；loose 意思不同，不能表示“失去工作”。
Q13. really best compared to before → really ______ compared to before

Key Grammar / 关键语法： comparative form
Why? / 为什么？
The phrase compares the system with “before”, so the comparative better is required.
句中有 compared to before，属于两者比较，因此用比较级 better。
Q14. has to sent her kids → has to ______ her kids

Key Grammar / 关键语法： has to + base verb

has to 后面接动词原形 send。
