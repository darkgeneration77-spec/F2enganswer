Form 2 English VS — Gapped Text Set 9

A Friendship to Cherish — Teacher’s Reference Version
Answer Key: 1H · 2G · 3F · 4D · 5B · 6A

Original Article / 原文

Diana and I were neighbours and went to the same primary school. 1. ______ We later attended different secondary schools but met during holidays. 2. ______ We also discussed lessons and chatted. Then Diana's family had to move to Putrajaya. 3. ______ We were like sisters. I would go North while she had to go South. 4. ______ On the last day before the move, Diana slept at my house. 5. ______ We laughed and cried. After a year we still missed each other. 6. ______ It was a short meeting but at least we were together.

Options A–H

A. We only met once when her family stopped on their way to her grandparents’ house.
B. We talked and reminisced about the past.
C. We could travel to the same destinations on our own.
D. The two ways were completely in opposite directions.
E. We could not talk so we just slept.
F. It was shattering to both of us.
G. We would meet every day either at my house or hers.
H. My mother, a housewife, would always be the one to fetch us from school.

Gap 1

Answer / 答案： H — My mother, a housewife, would always be the one to fetch us from school.
Key clue / 关键： same primary school
Why? / 为什么？
H continues the school routine.
H 延续小学生活，说明妈妈接她们放学。
Teacher Focus: 前句 + 后句 → pronoun reference → connector → time sequence → topic continuity.

Gap 2

Answer / 答案： G — We would meet every day either at my house or hers.
Key clue / 关键： holidays / made up for lost time
Why? / 为什么？
G explains they met every day during holidays.
G 说明假期天天见面。
Teacher Focus: 前句 + 后句 → pronoun reference → connector → time sequence → topic continuity.

Gap 3

Answer / 答案： F — It was shattering to both of us.
Key clue / 关键： family had to move
Why? / 为什么？
F expresses how upsetting the news was.
搬家消息让两人很难过，所以 F。
Teacher Focus: 前句 + 后句 → pronoun reference → connector → time sequence → topic continuity.

Gap 4

Answer / 答案： D — The two ways were completely in opposite directions.
Key clue / 关键： North vs South
Why? / 为什么？
D summarizes the opposite directions.
North 和 South 正是 opposite directions。
Teacher Focus: 前句 + 后句 → pronoun reference → connector → time sequence → topic continuity.

Gap 5

Answer / 答案： B — We talked and reminisced about the past.
Key clue / 关键： last night together
Why? / 为什么？
B describes talking and remembering the past.
最后一晚回忆过去，然后 laughed and cried。
Teacher Focus: 前句 + 后句 → pronoun reference → connector → time sequence → topic continuity.

Gap 6

Answer / 答案： A — We only met once when her family stopped on their way to her grandparents’ house.
Key clue / 关键： after a year / short meeting
Why? / 为什么？
A introduces the one brief meeting.
后句说 short meeting，所以前面 A 必须提到那次见面。
Teacher Focus: 前句 + 后句 → pronoun reference → connector → time sequence → topic continuity.
