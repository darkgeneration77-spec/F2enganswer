Question 1

Source / 原文Hayati tells Harum: “Sorry, I can’t meet up because I am going to Velocity Mall after my piano class. I forgot I need to get my dad a present for his birthday party this Sunday.”
Hayati wrote the message to
I. explain why she cannot see Harum
II. remind Harum to buy a gift for someone
III. invite Harum to go to Velocity Mall with her
Answer / 答案： I — explain why she cannot see Harum
Key clue / 关键线索： “Sorry, I can’t meet up because...”
Why this answer? / 为什么？
The main purpose of the message is to explain why Hayati cannot meet Harum. The reason is that she has to go to Velocity Mall to buy her father a birthday present.
这则信息的主要目的，是解释 Hayati 为什么不能和 Harum 见面。后面的 Velocity Mall 和买礼物只是“不能见面”的原因。
Why not the other options? / 为什么其他选项不对？
II. Hayati herself needs to buy the gift; she is not reminding Harum to buy one.
III. She says she cannot meet Harum; she does not invite Harum to the mall.
Reading skill / 阅读技巧： Identify the writer’s purpose. 找“写这则信息是为了什么”，不要只抓到其中一个细节。

Question 2

Source / 原文PAYUNG CAFE advert: “30% off”, “Dine at our cafe until 20rd September”, “Additional 10% off for takeaway”, “Find us at No. 21, Jalan Ampang, Kuala Lumpur.”
Payung Cafe is
I. located in Sabah
II. closing after 20rd September
III. giving 30% off to eat-in customers
Answer / 答案： III — giving 30% off to eat-in customers
Key clue / 关键线索： “30% off” + “Dine at our cafe...”
Why this answer? / 为什么？
The 30% discount is connected with dining at the cafe. The advert also clearly gives a Kuala Lumpur address.
广告中的 30% 折扣对应堂食，而且地址明确写在 Kuala Lumpur。
Why not the other options? / 为什么其他选项不对？
I. The cafe serves Sabah dishes, but it is located in Kuala Lumpur.
II. The advert says customers may dine there until 20 September; it does not state the cafe will close permanently after that date.
Reading skill / 阅读技巧： Separate product/theme from location. “Sabah dishes” 不等于“located in Sabah”。

Question 3

Source / 原文Pizza offer: “BUY ONE LARGE PIZZA, GET ONE REGULAR PIZZA FOR FREE!” Available on weekdays, from 10 a.m. to 5 p.m. only.
If you buy
I. a regular pizza, you get a large one free
II. a pizza after 5 p.m., you get another one free
III. a large pizza at 10 a.m., you get a smaller one free
Answer / 答案： III — a large pizza at 10 a.m., you get a smaller one free
Key clue / 关键线索： buy one LARGE pizza → get one REGULAR pizza free; 10 a.m.–5 p.m.
Why this answer? / 为什么？
A large pizza purchased at 10 a.m. qualifies for the offer, and the free pizza is a regular-sized one, which is smaller than a large pizza.
10 a.m. 在优惠时段内。买的是 large pizza，免费的是 regular pizza，因此第三项符合广告。
Why not the other options? / 为什么其他选项不对？
I. The offer works in the opposite direction: buy large, get regular free.
II. The offer is only valid until 5 p.m., not after 5 p.m.
Reading skill / 阅读技巧： Check both conditions: what must be bought + valid time. 两个条件都要同时符合。

Question 4

Source / 原文Fatin emails Kasri: “Tomorrow’s training — it’s not cancelled but we’ll be at the sports centre instead of training at school. It’ll be at 3 o’clock, as usual.”
Tomorrow, badminton training is
I. cancelled
II. at a different time
III. in a different place
Answer / 答案： III — in a different place
Key clue / 关键线索： “sports centre instead of training at school” + “3 o’clock, as usual”
Why this answer? / 为什么？
The time stays the same, but the venue changes from school to the sports centre.
时间还是 3 点，没有改变；改变的是地点，从学校改到 sports centre。
Why not the other options? / 为什么其他选项不对？
I. The email explicitly says “it’s not cancelled”.
II. It says “3 o’clock, as usual”, so the time is unchanged.
Reading skill / 阅读技巧： Contrast changed vs unchanged details. 看到 instead of 就要特别注意“换了什么”。

Question 5

Source / 原文Azulah texts Indra: “Can you tell Dad I won’t be home until after dinner tonight? I’ve got to take the dog to the vet this evening.”
Azulah is texting Indra to
I. apologise to her father
II. explain why she will be late tonight
III. remind Indra to take the cat to the vet
Answer / 答案： II — explain why she will be late tonight
Key clue / 关键线索： “I won’t be home until after dinner” + “I’ve got to take the dog to the vet”
Why this answer? / 为什么？
Azulah explains that she will arrive home late because she must take the dog to the vet.
Azulah 说明自己今晚会迟回家，并给出原因：她要带狗去看兽医。
Why not the other options? / 为什么其他选项不对？
I. She asks Indra to tell Dad, but she does not apologise.
III. Azulah herself will take the dog; it is not a cat, and she is not asking Indra to do it.
Reading skill / 阅读技巧： Track pronouns and who performs the action. 看清楚谁做什么，尤其是 I / you / Dad。

Question 6

Source / 原文Dentist reminder: “Your appointment with Dr Carlo is on Friday 1st July, at 11.30 a.m. If you need to cancel your appointment, you must phone or email at least 24 hours before your visit.”
The email reminds Ling Ming to
I. call Dr Carlo to make an appointment
II. email Dr Carlo before her appointment
III. tell Dr Carlo if she cannot go to the appointment
Answer / 答案： III — tell Dr Carlo if she cannot go to the appointment
Key clue / 关键线索： “If you need to cancel... you must phone or email at least 24 hours before”
Why this answer? / 为什么？
The appointment has already been made. The reminder is specifically about contacting the clinic if Ling Ming needs to cancel.
预约已经安排好了。电邮重点提醒的是：如果不能去，就必须提前通知诊所。
Why not the other options? / 为什么其他选项不对？
I. She does not need to make an appointment because the appointment already exists.
II. She only needs to email or phone if she wants to cancel; she does not have to email before every appointment.
Reading skill / 阅读技巧： Read conditional words carefully: IF changes the meaning. 看见 if，不要把“有条件才做”误读成“一定要做”。

Question 7

Source / 原文SUPER SWIM SCHOOL: “Sign up now for our next school holiday programme! Suitable for all levels of ability — from beginners to experts. Only thirteen children per class. Hurry!”
Super Swim School is
I. reminding people to book early to get a place
II. advising people that only strong swimmers can sign up
III. announcing that the swimming programme has started
Answer / 答案： I — reminding people to book early to get a place
Key clue / 关键线索： “Only thirteen children per class. Hurry!”
Why this answer? / 为什么？
Places are limited, so “Hurry!” tells readers to register early before the class fills up.
每班只有 13 个名额，“Hurry!” 表示名额有限，要尽早报名。
Why not the other options? / 为什么其他选项不对？
II. The programme is suitable for all levels, including beginners.
III. It says “next school holiday programme”, so the programme has not already started.
Reading skill / 阅读技巧： Use urgency words such as “Hurry!” as purpose clues. 这类广告词通常暗示尽快行动。

Question 8

Source / 原文WATERLAND THEME PARK: open 10 a.m.–6 p.m. daily; tickets RM50–RM90; “Enjoy RM10 off per person if you get a family or group ticket.”
The theme park is
I. announcing a change to its opening hours
II. promoting rides for families only
III. offering a group discount
Answer / 答案： III — offering a group discount
Key clue / 关键线索： “RM10 off per person if you get a family or group ticket”
Why this answer? / 为什么？
The advertisement directly offers a discount when visitors buy a family or group ticket.
广告直接说明购买 family / group ticket 时，每人可减 RM10，因此重点是团体折扣。
Why not the other options? / 为什么其他选项不对？
I. The advert states the opening hours but does not say they have changed.
II. The rides are open to the public; they are not for families only.
Reading skill / 阅读技巧： Distinguish information from purpose. 写出营业时间不等于“宣布营业时间改变”。
