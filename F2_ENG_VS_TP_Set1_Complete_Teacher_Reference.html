Paper 2 · Part 1 — Writing

Task: Write a note to a friend about an advertisement for a fitness club, about 80 words.

Suggested Answer / 建议答案
Dear Jacky, If you are looking for a fitness club, I highly recommend Power Fitness Club! They offer classes for all levels from beginner to advanced, so you can find the perfect workout no matter where you're at in your fitness journey. Also, their experienced instructors make sure you get the most out of your workout. Their coaching fees are very reasonable. On top of that, they even offer dancing lessons if you want to mix things up. Give it a try, you won't regret it. From, Ming (84 words)
Key / 关键： fitness club recommendation + classes for all levels + experienced instructors + reasonable fees + dancing lessons.
Why it works / 为什么： It directly informs the friend about the advertisement and adds relevant details in note format.
答案直接介绍广告内容，并加入课程、教练、收费和舞蹈课等相关资料，符合 note 的沟通目的。

Paper 2 · Part 2 — Writing

Suggested Answer / 建议答案
A very good morning to our Headmaster, Ms Fatimah, most respected teachers and beloved students. I, Jason Chong as the head prefect of SMK Taman Jaya would like to talk about ways to spend money wisely, in conjunction with Consumer Day in this lovely morning. Spending money wisely is important to avoid unnecessary debt and learn to save money. One way to do this is by preparing a budget to determine the total amount of money you can spend and provide a clearer picture of your financial situation. Another way is to avoid buying on impulse and instead let the idea sink for a few days before deciding to make the purchase. It's also important to prepare a shopping list before going out to help stay within budget and only bring cash to pay for purchases to avoid credit card debt. By following these tips, you can learn how to manage your money effectively and avoid financial stress. Thank you for listening. (113 words)
Key / 关键： speech opening + importance of wise spending + budget + avoid impulse buying + shopping list + cash + conclusion.
Why it works / 为什么： The suggested response uses a speech-style opening and closing and develops practical ways to spend money wisely.
范文使用演讲式开场和结尾，并具体说明预算、避免冲动消费、购物清单及现金付款等方法。
