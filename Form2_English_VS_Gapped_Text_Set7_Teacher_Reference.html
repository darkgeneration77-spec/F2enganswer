Form 2 English VS — Gapped Text Set 7

The Origin of the Archimedes’ Principle — Teacher’s Reference Version
Answer Key: 1A · 2F · 3C · 4D · 5H · 6B

Original Article / 原文

In ancient Greece, a king wondered whether his crown was solid gold. 1. ______ He suspected the goldsmith had used a cheaper metal. The king asked Archimedes to find out without damaging the crown. 2. ______ Archimedes studied the density problem. 3. ______ One day, water spilled out when he stepped into a bathtub. He had a great idea. 4. ______ He weighed the crown and compared the water displaced by the crown and a lump of gold of the same weight. 5. ______ More water spilled out with the crown. The crown had more metal than the lump of solid gold. 6. ______

Options A–H

A. The king often wondered whether his crown was made of solid gold.
B. He had found the answer to the king's question.
C. Archimedes thought about this problem all the time.
D. He was so excited that he ran out to the streets shouting “Eureka” which means “I have found it!”
E. He was also a great mathematician and was known to make many precise calculations.
F. This was no easy task but Archimedes accepted the challenge.
G. He engineers mirrors to focus the beams of the sun.
H. If the crown was solid gold, the amount of water that spilled out would be the same.

Gap 1

Answer / 答案： A — The king often wondered whether his crown was made of solid gold.
Key clue / 关键： king / crown
Why? / 为什么？
A introduces the king’s doubt.
A 先提出国王怀疑皇冠是否纯金。
Teacher Focus: 前句 + 后句 → pronoun reference → connector → time sequence → topic continuity.

Key clue / 关键： without damaging crown
Why? / 为什么？

F 的 This 回指“不损坏皇冠却要验证”的任务。
