Set 5 — How to protect our planet

Teacher’s Reference Version ｜ English + 华语 explanation

Original Passage / 原文

Green!? Keep our environment green!? These words have been repeated since our childhood, but is there anyone doing it? However it is really important for us and how can we do this? The climate around the world is changing rapidly and it is very scary. This will affect our health, daily life and environment. We need to take this issue seriously and learn how to protect our planet, our family and ourselves.

There are some ways that we can think about it. First, we must save electricity. By turning off the electronic devices when they are not needed. An example, we can switch off the lights and fans when we are not in the room. We can also replace electric heater to solar heater.

Other than saving electricity, we can also plant trees in our surrounding. We can buy some small pots and plant some vegetables. After the vegetables have grown, we can pluck them, cook and eat. It is natural and does not contain any chemical substances. This can also develop new hobbies such as music and sports. This will avoid wasting our time sitting in front the computer and do something unproductive. This will directly help us connect with our friends personally.

In conclusion, we must not only ‘think green’ but also ‘act green’ and ‘support green’.
