Paper 2 · Part 2 — Speech: Benefits of Extracurricular Activities

Suggested Answer / 建议答案
Good morning to Mrs Maha, teachers and fellow students. Today, the topic of my talk is on the benefits of extracurricular activities. These activities allow us to pursue our interests and passions. Clubs like Chess Club and Arts and Crafts Club cater to different interests. We get to socialise with like-minded people and make new friends. For instance, by joining Scouts and enjoying activities like camping and hiking. Furthermore, we learn that we must work hard and practise a lot if we want to win when we take part in sports. Finally, participating in extracurricular activities enables us to list our achievements in our resume. I strongly encourage everyone to join clubs and sports teams that interest them. Thank you. (109 words)
Key / 关键： interests + socialise + make friends + discipline/practice + achievements for resume.
