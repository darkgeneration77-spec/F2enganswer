Set 5 — Malaysia’s First Astronaut

Teacher’s Reference Version ｜ English + 华语 explanation

Original Passage / 原文

Dato’ Dr Sheikh Muszaphar Shukor Al Masrie bin Sheikh Mustapha is a Malaysian orthopaedic surgeon（骨科医生） and the first Malaysian astronaut（第一位马来西亚宇航员）. Sheikh Muszaphar was launched（发射） on 10 October 2007 to the International Space Station（国际空间站） (ISS) aboard Soyuz TMA-11（联盟号飞船） with the Expedition 16 crew（第16远征队）, through an agreement（协议） with Russia under the Angkasawan program（马来西亚太空人计划）. He then returned to Earth（返回地球） on 21 October 2007 aboard Soyuz TMA-10 with the Expedition 15 crew members（第15远征队成员）, Fyodor Yurchikhin and Oleg Kotov. Sheikh Muszaphar's space journey（太空旅程） began in early 2006. He and three other finalists（入围者） were chosen for the Malaysian Angkasawan spaceflight program（太空飞行计划）. After completing the initial training（初期训练） at Star City（星城） in Russia, Sheikh Muszaphar and Faiz Khaleed（法兹·卡利德） were selected to undergo an 18-month training program（18个月训练计划） in Russia. In the end, Sheikh Muszaphar was selected as the prime crew member（主要成员）, while Faiz Khaleed served as a back-up（后备）. Then, on 17 September, after the final medical tests（最终体检） and training examinations（训练考核）, it was announced（宣布） that Sheikh Muszaphar would be flying on the Soyuz TMA-11 mission（任务）. Soyuz TMA-11 was successfully launched（成功发射） at 13:22 UTC on Wednesday, 10 October 2007. It carried Whitson, Malenchenko and Sheikh Muszaphar. While on board the International Space Station, Sheikh Muszaphar performed experiments（实验） related to liver cancer（肝癌）, leukaemia cells（白血病细胞）, and the crystallisation（结晶） of various proteins（蛋白质） and microbes（微生物） in space. These experiments will benefit general science（普通科学） and medical research（医学研究）. Meanwhile, the experiments related to the crystallisation of proteins known as lipases（脂肪酶） will directly benefit local industries（本地工业）. Lipases are types of protein enzymes（蛋白酶） used in the manufacturing（制造） of products such as textiles（纺织品） and cosmetics（化妆品）. After spending 10 days（10天） in space, Sheikh Muszaphar boarded Soyuz TMA-10 for his return journey（回程） to Earth.
