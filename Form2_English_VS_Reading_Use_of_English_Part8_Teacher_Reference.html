Question 1

Source / 原文： Yatin emails Elain: “I will not be able to attend next week’s baking class. I will be attending my brother’s wedding. Could you please send me the video of the lesson instead?”
From the email, we know that Yatin
A. can teach Elain how to bake cakes
B. will be getting married next week
C. wants the video of the baking lesson
Answer / 答案： C — wants the video of the baking lesson
Key clue / 关键： Could you please send me the video of the lesson instead?
Why? / 为什么？
Yatin directly asks for the lesson video because she cannot attend the class.
Yatin 因为不能上课，所以直接请求对方把 baking lesson 的视频发给她。
Why are the others wrong? / 其他选项为什么错？
A: Nothing says Yatin teaches Elain.
B: It is her brother who is getting married.
Teacher Focus / 教学重点： Direct request: look for Could you please...?

Question 2

Source / 原文： George Town Book Club advert: “We would like to invite you to join us on our exciting journey through the world of books!”
The purpose of the advertisement is to
A. appeal to people to join the Book Club
B. ask people to pay the annual membership fee
C. appeal to people to visit Cannon Bookstore
Answer / 答案： A — appeal to people to join the Book Club
Key clue / 关键： invite you to join us
Why? / 为什么？
The main purpose is recruitment into the Book Club. The fee and venue are supporting details.
广告主旨是邀请人们加入 Book Club；会费和地点只是附加资料。
Why are the others wrong? / 其他选项为什么错？
B/C are details, not the main purpose.
Teacher Focus / 教学重点： Purpose questions: choose the broadest option covering the whole advert.

Question 3

Source / 原文： Dad tells Ling Ling: “water the plants in the afternoon and lock the gates at night.”
At night, Ling Ling needs to
A. water the plants
B. lock the gates
C. cook her dinner
Answer / 答案： B — lock the gates
Key clue / 关键： lock the gates at night
Why? / 为什么？
The time phrase directly matches the required action.
题目问 at night，原文对应动作就是 lock the gates。
Why are the others wrong? / 其他选项为什么错？
A happens in the afternoon.
C She is given money to order takeaway, not cook.
Teacher Focus / 教学重点： Match time phrase to action.

Question 4

Source / 原文： First Aid Workshop advert: “Learn to give people emergency treatment. You could save a life.”
The advertisement aims to
A. give people emergency treatment
B. save lives
C. tell people about the workshop
Answer / 答案： C — tell people about the workshop
Key clue / 关键： First Aid Workshop + venue/date/register
Why? / 为什么？
The advertisement’s function is to inform and attract people to the workshop. Emergency treatment and saving lives describe what participants may learn or achieve.
广告本身的目的，是告诉人们有这个 workshop 并吸引报名；急救和救人是课程内容/效果。
Why are the others wrong? / 其他选项为什么错？
A/B describe workshop outcomes, not the advert’s communicative purpose.
Teacher Focus / 教学重点： Distinguish advert purpose from programme benefit.

Question 5

Source / 原文： Message to Adan: “We are having a farewell party for our class teacher... We hope you can join us to bid farewell to our beloved teacher.”
The purpose of the message is to
A. ask Adan to attend the farewell party
B. say farewell to Adan
C. ask Adan to say goodbye to his teacher
Answer / 答案： A — ask Adan to attend the farewell party
Key clue / 关键： We hope you can join us
Why? / 为什么？
The classmates are inviting Adan to the farewell party.
同学们是在邀请 Adan 参加 farewell party。
Why are the others wrong? / 其他选项为什么错？
B: Adan is not the person leaving.
C: The message is about attending the event, not merely saying goodbye separately.
Teacher Focus / 教学重点： Invitation purpose.

Question 6

Source / 原文： Silver Screen Cinema offer: “GET A FREE REGULAR POPCORN FOR EVERY PURCHASE OF A LARGE POPCORN SET.”
We can get a free regular popcorn if we
A. purchase a large drink
B. purchase a large popcorn set
C. purchase a large popcorn set and a large drink
Answer / 答案： B — purchase a large popcorn set
Key clue / 关键： FREE REGULAR POPCORN ... PURCHASE OF A LARGE POPCORN SET
Why? / 为什么？
Only the large popcorn set is required for the free regular popcorn.
要得到免费 regular popcorn，只需要购买 large popcorn set。
Why are the others wrong? / 其他选项为什么错？
A relates to the free drink offer.
C adds an unnecessary condition.
Teacher Focus / 教学重点： Promotions: do not add extra conditions.

Question 7

Source / 原文： Steven emails Cindy after her graduation: “Both Mum and Dad are really proud of you... Love, Steven.”
Based on the email, Steven is most likely Cindy's
A. sister
B. brother
C. mother
Answer / 答案： B — brother
Key clue / 关键： Both Mum and Dad + sender name Steven
Why? / 为什么？
Steven speaks of “Mum and Dad” as shared parents, and the supplied key identifies him as Cindy’s brother.
Steven 把 Mum 和 Dad 当作共同父母来提，答案表判断他是 Cindy 的 brother。
Why are the others wrong? / 其他选项为什么错？
A conflicts with the male name/context selected by the key.
C would not refer to herself as Mum in this way.
Teacher Focus / 教学重点： Family relationship inference.

Question 8

Source / 原文： Library notice: “the library will be closed for two months due to renovation work.”
The library
A. will be taken care of by Puan Azrinal Mazhar
B. will be closed for two weeks
C. will be undergoing repairs
Answer / 答案： C — will be undergoing repairs
Key clue / 关键： due to renovation work
Why? / 为什么？
Renovation means repair/improvement work, so the library will be undergoing repairs.
renovation work 表示翻修、维修，所以图书馆会进行 repairs。
Why are the others wrong? / 其他选项为什么错？
A Puan Azrinal is the signer, not necessarily the caretaker.
B says two weeks, while the notice says two months.
Teacher Focus / 教学重点： Paraphrase: renovation work = repairs.
