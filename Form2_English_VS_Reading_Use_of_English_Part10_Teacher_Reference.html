Question 1

Source / 原文： Vivian writes to Kaven: “You asked for advice in preparing to study in London. To get a student visa... Next, start shopping for the clothes...”
What is the purpose of Vivian's letter?
A. To apply for a student visa.
B. To tell Kaven she is studying overseas
C. To advise Kaven on how to prepare for studying overseas
Answer / 答案： C — To advise Kaven on how to prepare for studying overseas
Key clue / 关键： You asked for advice in preparing to study in London
Why? / 为什么？
Vivian gives Kaven practical preparation advice about visas and clothing.
Vivian 提供签证和衣服方面的准备建议，所以目的是指导 Kaven 如何准备海外留学。
Why are the others wrong? / 其他选项为什么错？
A Kaven, not Vivian, would apply.
B The letter is advice, not an announcement about Vivian.
Teacher Focus / 教学重点： Purpose from opening sentence.

Question 2

Source / 原文： Dialogue: “The police are looking into Jeff’s murder.”
By the phrase looking into, the man means
A. looking at
B. investigating
C. looking for Tom
Answer / 答案： B — investigating
Key clue / 关键： police + murder
Why? / 为什么？
“Look into” means investigate or examine a matter carefully.
look into 在这个语境中表示调查，尤其搭配 police 和 murder。
Why are the others wrong? / 其他选项为什么错？
A is literal and too weak.
C mentions Tom, who is not in the sentence.
Teacher Focus / 教学重点： Phrasal verb in context.

Question 3

Source / 原文： Sri Amah Hypermarket offer: Ninio’s Hazelnut Coffee, 40 sachets per packet, “Only 2 per customer”.
Each customer can buy
A. 40 sachets only
B. two packets only
C. any amount while stocks last
Answer / 答案： B — two packets only
Key clue / 关键： Only 2 per customer
Why? / 为什么？
The limit applies to packets, and each customer may buy only two.
限制写明每位顾客只能买 2 包。40 sachets 是每包数量，不是购买上限。
Why are the others wrong? / 其他选项为什么错？
A confuses contents per packet with purchase limit.
C contradicts the limit.
Teacher Focus / 教学重点： Separate package size from purchase limit.

Question 4

Source / 原文： Pie chart: One child 10%; Two children 20%; Three children 30%; Four children 25%; More than four children 15%.
The pie chart above shows that more families have
A. only one child
B. one or two children
C. more than two children
Answer / 答案： C — more than two children
Key clue / 关键： 3+ children = 30% + 25% + 15% = 70%
Why? / 为什么？
Families with more than two children make up 70%, which is the majority.
超过两个孩子的家庭包括 3、4、4以上，共 70%，明显最多。
Why are the others wrong? / 其他选项为什么错？
A = 10%.
B = 10% + 20% = 30%.
Teacher Focus / 教学重点： Combine categories before comparing percentages.

Question 5

Source / 原文： Insect spray label: “Poison! Keep away from children.”
The label makes it very clear that the spray
A. is poisonous
B. kills all types of insects
C. must be sprayed on insects
Answer / 答案： A — is poisonous
Key clue / 关键： Poison!
Why? / 为什么？
The label directly states that the product is poisonous.
标签直接写 Poison，因此答案就是 is poisonous。
Why are the others wrong? / 其他选项为什么错？
B/C are not stated by the label.
Teacher Focus / 教学重点： Use explicit warning words.

Question 6

Source / 原文： NOKI 666 advert: slim rectangular touchscreen device, “Chic, lightweight and easy to use. Captures and shows images... Buy before 20 June...”
The advertisement is promoting
A. a camera
B. a special offer
C. a mobile phone
Answer / 答案： C — a mobile phone
Key clue / 关键： NOKI 666 device + phone-like design
Why? / 为什么？
The product shown and described is a mobile phone. The special price is only a promotional detail.
广告主要推广的是 mobile phone，优惠价只是附加促销信息。
Why are the others wrong? / 其他选项为什么错？
A The device is not presented as a standalone camera.
B A special offer exists, but it is not the product itself.
Teacher Focus / 教学重点： Product vs promotion: identify what is being sold.

Question 7

Source / 原文： Notice: “Attend the workshop on How to Score in PT3. It gives tips on passing with flying colours in PT3.”
The notice above should interest
A. all secondary students
B. students sitting for PT3
C. students sitting for final exams
Answer / 答案： B — students sitting for PT3
Key clue / 关键： How to Score in PT3
Why? / 为什么？
The workshop is specifically designed for students preparing for PT3.
workshop 专门讲 PT3，因此最相关的是正在应考 PT3 的学生。
Why are the others wrong? / 其他选项为什么错？
A is too broad.
C does not specify PT3.
Teacher Focus / 教学重点： Target audience from title.

Question 8

Source / 原文： TV listing: “Jom & Jen COMEBACK DUET... first concert together after nine years.”
The television programme is likely to be a
A. live concert
B. family drama
C. singing competition
Answer / 答案： A — live concert
Key clue / 关键： COMEBACK DUET + first concert together
Why? / 为什么？
The listing describes a concert performance by a singing duet.
节目介绍的是二人组合 comeback concert，所以最可能是 live concert。
Why are the others wrong? / 其他选项为什么错？
B/C are not supported by the programme description.
Teacher Focus / 教学重点： Genre identification from keywords.
