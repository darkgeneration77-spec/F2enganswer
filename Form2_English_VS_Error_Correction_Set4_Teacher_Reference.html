Answers & Explanations / 答案与解释

(a) awareness → aware
After be, an adjective is needed: be aware of/to...
be 后面要用形容词，awareness 是名词，aware 才对。
(b) theirs → their
A possessive adjective is needed before the noun feelings.
feelings 前面要用形容词性物主代词 their，不是独立使用的 theirs。
(c) offering → offer
After modal verb can, use the base form of the verb.
can 后面一定接动词原形，所以用 offer。
(d) you're → your
Your shows possession before the noun friends.
friends 前面表示“你的”，应使用 your；you're = you are。
(e) actualy → actually
This is a spelling correction. Actually has double l.
拼写错误：actually 有两个 l。
(f) shuts → shut
The subject is they, so use the base-form present verb shut.
主语是 they，不能用第三人称单数 shuts，要用 shut。
(g) by → through
The correct phrase is go through a hard time.
固定搭配：go through a hard time = 经历艰难时期。
(h) their → them
After the verb cheer, an object pronoun is needed: cheer them up.
cheer 后面要接宾格 them，固定搭配是 cheer them up。
(i) therefore → and
The sentence joins two parallel verbs: help and comfort.
这里连接 help 和 comfort 两个并列动作，用 and。
(j) hanging → hang
After doesn’t have to, use the base form: hang out.
have to 后面接动词原形，所以是 hang out。
Quick Rule / 口诀： modal + V1；be + adjective；possessive adjective + noun；verb + object pronoun；固定搭配要整体看，不要只盯一个词。
