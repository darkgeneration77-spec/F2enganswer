Source / 原题资料： Melissa thanks Grandma for an e-book reader and says she really appreciates it.
Q1. Melissa wants her grandmother to know
A. that she likes reading e-books
B. that she appreciates the gift very much
C. that her mother knows how to buy e-books
Answer / 答案： B — that she appreciates the gift very much
Key clue / 关键： “I really appreciate this e-book reader”
Why? / 为什么？
The main purpose is to thank Grandma and show appreciation for the birthday gift.
主要目的就是感谢 Grandma，并表达她非常珍惜这份生日礼物。
Why not the others? / 其他选项为什么错？
A: She likes reading, but that is only supporting detail.
C: Her mother buying more e-books is not the message purpose.
Teacher Focus / 教学重点： Main purpose.

Answer / 答案： C — have an opportunity to join an important competition
Key clue / 关键： Annual Inter-School Singing Competition
Why? / 为什么？
The club is preparing members for an annual inter-school competition, which is the major opportunity offered.
club 的重点是训练成员参加 Annual Inter-School Singing Competition，因此 C 最完整。
Why not the others? / 其他选项为什么错？
A: It meets every Friday, not every weekend.
B: 2–4 p.m. is afternoon, not evening.
Teacher Focus / 教学重点： Paraphrase competition information.

Part 2 — Error Correction: A Shopping Trip

Answers: 9 them · 10 a · 11 and · 12 tried · 13 decided · 14 to · 15 had · 16 for

Q9. drove they to the mall → drove ______
Answer / 答案： them
Key clue / 关键： object pronoun
Why? / 为什么？
After the verb drove, use the object pronoun them.
drove 后面需要宾格 them。
Teacher Focus / 教学重点： they = subject; them = object.
Q10. only an few customers → only ______ few customers
Answer / 答案： a
Key clue / 关键： fixed quantifier
Why? / 为什么？
The correct phrase is a few customers.
固定结构是 a few customers。
Teacher Focus / 教学重点： a few + plural noun.
Q11. tried out the blouses so asked → ______ asked
Answer / 答案： and
Key clue / 关键： addition/sequence
Why? / 为什么？
She tried the blouses and asked for her mother’s opinion; these are sequential actions, not cause-result.
她试了 blouse，然后询问妈妈意见，是顺接关系，所以用 and。
Teacher Focus / 教学重点： and vs so.
Q12. she try on some skirts → she ______
Answer / 答案： tried
Key clue / 关键： past tense
Why? / 为什么？
The whole shopping trip is narrated in the past, so use tried.
整段是过去时，所以用 tried。
Teacher Focus / 教学重点： Tense consistency.
Q13. They decides to go → They ______
Answer / 答案： decided
Key clue / 关键： past tense + plural subject
Why? / 为什么？
Past tense decided fits the narrative; “decides” is present third-person singular.
过去叙事用 decided；decides 是现在时第三人称单数。
Teacher Focus / 教学重点： Past narrative.
Q14. went by look for shoes → went ______ look
Answer / 答案： to
Key clue / 关键： to + base verb
Why? / 为什么？
Use to to express purpose: went to look for shoes.
went to look 表示“去寻找”，所以用 to。
Teacher Focus / 教学重点： Purpose: go to + V1.
Q15. they have lunch → they ______ lunch
Answer / 答案： had
Key clue / 关键： past tense
Why? / 为什么？
The shopping trip happened last weekend, so use had.
事情发生在 last weekend，因此用 had。
Teacher Focus / 教学重点： Past tense.
Q16. had ice cream as dessert → had ice cream ______ dessert
Answer / 答案： for
Key clue / 关键： collocation
Why? / 为什么？
The supplied answer scheme uses for dessert.
答案表使用固定搭配 for dessert。
Teacher Focus / 教学重点： for breakfast/lunch/dinner/dessert.
