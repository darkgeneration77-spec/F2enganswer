Original Passage / 原文

Jamaican sprinter Usain Bolt, the first man in Olympic History who won both the 100m and 200m races in world record times. This happened at the 2008 Beijing Olympics. 2012, London Olympics, he created a miracle and won gold medals in the 100m and 200m races. Four years later, once again he achieved gold medals in the 100m and 200m races during Rio Olympics. In history, he is the first man who set three world records in a single Olympic Games Competition.

Usain Bolt also sets his record in the 2009 World Championships in Berlin which was 100m in 9.58 seconds and the 200m in 19.19 seconds. In 2012, he finished 4 × 100m in just 36.84 which was also a new world record. This was beyond human limit.

Usain St Leo Bolt was born on 21 August 1986. He is 195 cm tall and 94 kg. He studied at William Knibb Memorial High School at Jamaica.

He has his own representative watch made by Hublot, his own clothing line in conjunction with Puma, his own headphone range ‘Soul by Usain Bolt’, his own restaurant ‘Tracks & Records’. He also created a foundation named Usain Bolt Foundation to help the charities such as underprivileged children, special needs children and children who are interested in sports.
