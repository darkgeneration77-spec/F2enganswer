Error Correction · Set 3

Form 2 English · Teacher’s Reference Version · English + 华语解释

Original Text 原题

Shopping trips should be planned and prepared with a list of things you want to buy. Make sure everything you are purchasing is according to yours list so you don’t buy on impulse. Make sure that you have taken the amount of money that you need for the things you want to buy. Don’t over-withdraw your money and sticking to your budget. You will be enticed to spend more than your budget, therefore you shouldn’t have extra money in your wallet and purse. All the prices of the items should be checked before buying. Keep doing surveys such as waiting for promotions and sales, shop at places that offer the cheapest price and compare the quality of each shops. In order to avoid buying extra items, you can try to carried a shopping basket instead of pushing a supermarket trolley. This way will ensure that you will only bought items that are on your shopping list. Last but not least, do not shop with an empty stomach because you were more likely to buy more item.

Example: prepares → prepared

Answers & Explanations 答案与解释

(a) want → wanted
Parallel past participle: planned and prepared with a list of things you wanted to buy.
这里叙述已准备好的购物清单，动词时态配合语境。
(b) sticking → stick
After “Don’t”, use the base verb: Don’t over-withdraw ... and stick to your budget.
Don’t + 原形动词，所以 sticking 改成 stick。
(c) therefore → so
The second clause gives the result/advice. “..., so you shouldn’t ...” links the clauses naturally.
前面是原因，后面是结果/建议，用 so 连接。
(d) and → or
“wallet or purse” presents alternatives.
wallet 和 purse 是选择关系，这里用 or。
(e) waiting → wait
After “such as”, this list continues with action advice; use the base form consistently with “shop” and “compare”.
与后面的 shop、compare 保持动作形式一致。
(f) shops → shop
“each” takes a singular countable noun: each shop.
each + 单数名词，所以 shops → shop。
(g) carried → carry
“try to” + base verb: try to carry.
try to + 动词原形，所以用 carry。
(h) bought → buy
“will” + base verb: will only buy.
will + 动词原形，所以 bought → buy。
(i) were → are
This is general advice in the present tense: you are more likely to...
这是一般性的建议，用现在时 are。
(j) item → items
“more” requires a plural countable noun here: more items.
more + 可数名词复数，所以 item → items。

快速口诀

Don’t / will / to → 看动词原形；each → 看单数；more → 看复数；连接词 → 先判断前后逻辑。

不要只盯着错误词本身。先看它左边和右边的“语法触发词”，通常答案就在那里。
