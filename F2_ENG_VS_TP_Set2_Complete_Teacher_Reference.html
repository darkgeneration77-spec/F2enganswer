Paper 2 · Part 1 — Email

Task: In about 80 words, write an email to your cousin Azizah giving her advice about whether to accept an offer to study at a boarding school far from home.

Suggested Answer / 建议答案
Hi Azizah, How are you? I know you feel sad that the boarding school is far from home. You should accept the offer as it is a once-in-a-lifetime opportunity. With the school's excellent academic record, you can receive a good education. Don't worry. I'm sure you can make new friends. You will also be taught by experienced teachers who can guide you. Do take up the offer. It's a wonderful opportunity. If I'm given a place there, I would definitely accept it. Siti
Key / 关键： acknowledge her concern + clear advice + academic record + new friends + experienced teachers + strong conclusion.
Why? / 为什么？
The answer directly responds to Azizah's doubt and gives reasons for accepting the offer.
答案直接回应 Azizah 的犹豫，并用学校成绩、朋友及老师等理由支持建议。

Paper 2 · Part 2 — School Trip Essay

Task: In about 120 words, write an essay about a school trip your class had planned.

Suggested Answer / 建议答案
Last month our class went on a trip to Pulau Pinang. It was a three day trip. Five of us organised it. Here is what we did. First we did some research on the places that we could go to. We drew up a list of the places and worked out the cost for each person. Then the list was sent to everyone, including our teachers, for them to consider. Most students voted to go to Pulau Pinang. Next, we worked on accommodation. The place had to fit all 27 of us. Fortunately, we found an affordable place on the beach. That was perfect for us. We also had to hire a bus to take us from Ipoh to the island. After asking around, we located Mr Rama. Our teachers worked out the rates with him. Once all of that was settled, we had a briefing for everyone. Our teachers reminded everyone to get the permission forms signed. They also told us what to do and not to do during the trip. It was a successful trip because everyone was very cooperative. I hope that we can plan another trip next year.
Key / 关键： destination + duration + organisers + research + voting + accommodation + transport + briefing + permission slips + conclusion.
Why? / 为什么？
The essay follows the planning sequence given in the notes and develops each stage clearly.
范文依照题目给出的策划顺序展开：地点、投票、住宿、交通、讲解、家长签名和总结。
