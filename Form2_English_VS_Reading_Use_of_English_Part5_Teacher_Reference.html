Question 1

Source / 原文： Mei Lan emails Alita: “I heard that you were a little short of cash needed to attend the fair. As such, I’ve found you some work to do to earn you a little cash. Mr Ho needs someone to walk his dogs and has promised to pay some money for the task.”
The purpose of the email is to tell Alita
A. about a job she can do to earn some money
B. to walk Mr Ho's dogs
C. that Mei Lan is short of cash
Answer / 答案： A — about a job she can do to earn some money
Key clue / 关键： I’ve found you some work to do to earn you a little cash
Why? / 为什么？
The whole email is about informing Alita of a paid job opportunity. Walking Mr Ho’s dogs is the specific job, but the broader purpose is to tell her about work she can do to earn money.
整封电邮的主要目的，是告诉 Alita 有一份可以赚钱的工作。遛狗只是这份工作的具体内容，不是整封电邮的主旨。
Why are the others wrong? / 其他选项为什么错？
B is too narrow because it gives only the specific task.
C is wrong because Alita, not Mei Lan, is short of cash.
Teacher Focus / 教学重点： Main-purpose questions: choose the option that covers the whole message, not just one detail.

Question 2

Source / 原文： SMART TUITION CENTRE PT3 WORKSHOP: “We will be offering an intensive study programme to prepare students for their PT3 exams.”
Smart Tuition Centre is
A. holding the PT3 exams
B. offering a workshop to prepare students for the PT3 exams
C. encouraging students to exercise
Answer / 答案： B — offering a workshop to prepare students for the PT3 exams
Key clue / 关键： PT3 WORKSHOP + prepare students for their PT3 exams
Why? / 为什么？
The advertisement clearly promotes a study workshop to help students prepare for PT3.
广告明确宣传一个帮助学生准备 PT3 的 workshop，所以答案是 B。
Why are the others wrong? / 其他选项为什么错？
A: The centre is not conducting the official exam.
C: The word “exercises” refers to study exercises, not physical exercise.
Teacher Focus / 教学重点： Do not confuse academic “exercises” with physical exercise.

Question 3

Source / 原文： Ava writes to Carl: “I heard that you had won the Public Speaking Competition last week. I am very happy for you. Congratulations!”
Ava is writing to Carl to
A. console him
B. congratulate him
C. wish him good luck
Answer / 答案： B — congratulate him
Key clue / 关键： won the competition + Congratulations!
Why? / 为什么？
Because Carl has already won, Ava is congratulating him on his achievement.
Carl 已经赢得比赛，Ava 写信是为了祝贺他，因此用 congratulate。
Why are the others wrong? / 其他选项为什么错？
A: Console is used when someone is sad or has suffered a loss.
C: Good luck is normally said before an event, not after winning.
Teacher Focus / 教学重点： Use time clues: before event = wish good luck; after success = congratulate.

Question 4

Source / 原文： Charly messages Hisham: “Could you please head over to the grocery store to buy two cartons of eggs for us? Also, Dad would like you to get some butter... He is planning on making some butter cookies for Mum’s birthday.”
Charly is Hisham's
A. cousin
B. sister
C. aunt
Answer / 答案： B — sister
Key clue / 关键： Dad + Mum are shared family references
Why? / 为什么？
Charly refers to “Dad” and “Mum” in a way that implies she and Hisham share the same parents, so the supplied answer key identifies her as his sister.
Charly 直接用 Dad 和 Mum 指同一对父母，答案表因此判断她是 Hisham 的 sister。
Why are the others wrong? / 其他选项为什么错？
A/C are not supported by the family wording used in the message.
Teacher Focus / 教学重点： Inference from family terms: notice shared references like Mum, Dad, our.

Question 5

Source / 原文： SMK Taman Ehsan Teachers’ Day Celebration: Venue: Assembly Hall. Date: 10 June. Time: 9.30 a.m. – 1.30 p.m.
To attend the celebration, students should arrive at school by
A. 9.30 a.m.
B. 11.00 a.m.
C. 1.30 p.m.
Answer / 答案： A — 9.30 a.m.
Key clue / 关键： Time: 9.30 a.m. – 1.30 p.m.
Why? / 为什么？
The event starts at 9.30 a.m., so students should be there by that time.
活动 9.30 a.m. 开始，因此学生最迟应该在 9.30 a.m. 到达。
Why are the others wrong? / 其他选项为什么错？
B is after the event has already started.
C is the ending time.
Teacher Focus / 教学重点： Distinguish start time from end time.

Question 6

Source / 原文： Chinese New Year Special Event: “Children will receive red packets containing money; Light refreshments will be served; Lucky Draw with attractive prizes!; Fun games!”
During the event, visitors
A. can play games
B. must join the lucky draw
C. should bring their own food and drink
Answer / 答案： A — can play games
Key clue / 关键： Fun games!
Why? / 为什么？
The poster directly states that games are part of the event, so visitors can play them.
海报直接列出 Fun games，因此来宾可以参加游戏。
Why are the others wrong? / 其他选项为什么错？
B: The lucky draw is available, but the poster does not say everyone must join.
C: Refreshments will be served, so there is no instruction to bring food.
Teacher Focus / 教学重点： Modal verbs matter: can ≠ must. 注意“可以”和“必须”的差别。

Question 7

Source / 原文： Clinton writes: “Derby will not be able to attend your play as she has broken her hand and was admitted to the hospital. However, she asked me to take a video of your play so that she can watch it later on.”
The purpose of the message is to tell Jonathan
A. to pay Derby a visit soon
B. that Derby will not be attending his play
C. to record a video of his play
Answer / 答案： B — that Derby will not be attending his play
Key clue / 关键： Derby will not be able to attend your play
Why? / 为什么？
The message mainly informs Jonathan that Derby cannot attend. The video request is an extra detail explaining what Derby would like instead.
这则信息的主要目的是告诉 Jonathan：Derby 不能来看他的演出。录影只是附带说明。
Why are the others wrong? / 其他选项为什么错？
A: Clinton hopes Jonathan may visit Derby, but that is not the main purpose.
C: Clinton himself was asked to take the video.
Teacher Focus / 教学重点： Separate main purpose from secondary requests/details.

Question 8

Source / 原文： Mr Yong emails Tian Yang: “Your piano lesson has been cancelled this week. Do remember to keep on practising without me. I will contact you soon to arrange a replacement lesson.”
Mr Yong wants Tian Yang to
A. cancel his piano lesson
B. contact him as soon as possible
C. practise playing the piano by himself
Answer / 答案： C — practise playing the piano by himself
Key clue / 关键： keep on practising without me
Why? / 为什么？
Mr Yong directly tells Tian Yang to continue practising even though the lesson is cancelled.
Mr Yong 明确叫 Tian Yang 在没有老师的情况下继续练琴，所以答案是 C。
Why are the others wrong? / 其他选项为什么错？
A: The lesson is already cancelled by Mr Yong.
B: Mr Yong says he will contact Tian Yang, not the other way around.
Teacher Focus / 教学重点： Track who is expected to perform each action.
