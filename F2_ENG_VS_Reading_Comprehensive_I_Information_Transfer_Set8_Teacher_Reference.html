Set 8 — Khairul

Teacher’s Reference Version ｜ 英文 + 华语解释

Original Passage / 原文

Khairul is in Standard Six. He studied at Sekolah Rendah Sultan Abdul Arshad. His school is located about ten kilometres away from the nearest town. He has to wake up at 6.45am and take a bus because he has to leave very early as his home is very far from the school. He lives in Kampung Chempaka in Perak.

Khairul has 4 family members. Both of his parents are farmers and they are very poor. Their daily income is from planting the paddy. If the weather is bad, they can’t get any income at all. He has one younger brother and one younger sister, named Ishak and Marina, who are still too young to go to school.

Khairul has an ambition. He wants to become a lawyer to help his family live a more comfortable life. He is a kid who has high self-discipline. He is also very hardworking and gets good results in his examinations.

Khairul has filial piety. In the afternoon he goes to the farm and helps his parents. So he can reduce the work of his parents. He does his homework after helping his parents. He has to study by using an oil lamp because there is no electricity in Kampung Chempaka.
