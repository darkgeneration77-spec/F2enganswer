Q1. The open house is held
A. to celebrate Chinese New Year
B. for one week
C. for the residents of Mutiara Apartments
Answer / 答案： C — for the residents of Mutiara Apartments
Key clue / 关键： “All the residents are welcome to the event.”
Why? / 为什么？
The notice is issued by the Residents’ Association of Mutiara Apartments and welcomes all residents. It is held one week from today, not for one whole week.
告示由 Mutiara Apartments 居民协会发出，而且欢迎所有居民参加。one week from today 是“一周后的那一天”，不是活动持续一周。
Why not the others? / 其他选项为什么错？
A: The celebration is for Hari Raya Haji, not Chinese New Year.
B: “One week from today” refers to when it will be held, not its duration.
Teacher Focus / 教学重点： Watch time expressions: from today ≠ for one week.
Q2. The discount is given
A. to all customers of the restaurant
B. in celebration of Father's Day
C. when we order main courses
Answer / 答案： B — in celebration of Father's Day
Key clue / 关键： “Father’s Day Special Offer”
Why? / 为什么？
The 30% discount is presented as a Father’s Day special promotion. Main courses are the discounted items, but the occasion is Father’s Day.
广告标题直接写 Father’s Day Special Offer，因此折扣是为了庆祝父亲节。main courses 是折扣对象，不是出题问的“原因/场合”。
Why not the others? / 其他选项为什么错？
A: The advert does not say every customer automatically receives it.
C: Main courses are what receive the discount, not the occasion for the promotion.
Teacher Focus / 教学重点： Distinguish promotion occasion from promotion condition.
Q3. The email is

Key clue / 关键： “Please do contact me if it is your wallet”
Why? / 为什么？
Jack is not certain the wallet belongs to Edward, so he asks Edward to confirm ownership.
Jack 只是认为钱包“可能”属于 Edward，因此要 Edward 确认是不是自己的。
Why not the others? / 其他选项为什么错？
A: Jack does not know for certain that Edward lost it.
B: He does not ask Edward to go to the MRT station.
Teacher Focus / 教学重点： Notice uncertainty words such as may belong.
