Form 2 English VS — Gapped Text Set 3

Going to the Cinema — Teacher’s Reference Version
Answer Key: 1E · 2H · 3B · 4G · 5C · 6D

Original Article / 原文

Melissa, Alyssa and Ting decided to watch a movie at GSC, IOI City Mall. 1. ______ Then they bought tickets for the 2 p.m. show. There was a long queue. 2. ______ Finally, it was their turn. By the time they finished, it was almost 12 noon. 3. ______ They had lunch at a café. 4. ______ They wanted popcorn but the line was long. After 15 minutes they got their popcorn. 5. ______ They then waited to enter the cinema hall. They handed their tickets to the usher and walked towards the cinema hall. 6. ______

Options A–H

A. The girls walked in line to buy popcorn.
B. They decided to have lunch before the movie.
C. They bought a large popcorn to share.
D. There were many people at the cinema hall.
E. The girls met at IOI City Mall around 11 a.m.
F. They then rushed to the cinema.
G. After lunch, the girls headed towards the cinema.
H. The girls waited patiently to buy their tickets.

Gap 1

Answer / 答案： E — The girls met at IOI City Mall around 11 a.m.
Key clue / 关键： before buying tickets
Why? / 为什么？
E establishes where/when they met.
E 先交代约 11 点在商场见面。
Teacher Focus: 前句 + 后句 → pronoun reference → connector → time sequence → topic continuity.

Gap 2

Answer / 答案： H — The girls waited patiently to buy their tickets.
Key clue / 关键： long queue / finally their turn
Why? / 为什么？
H explains what happened while waiting.
前面排长龙，后面 finally 轮到她们，中间就是耐心等。
Teacher Focus: 前句 + 后句 → pronoun reference → connector → time sequence → topic continuity.

Gap 3

Answer / 答案： B — They decided to have lunch before the movie.
Key clue / 关键： almost 12 noon
Why? / 为什么？
B explains the decision to eat before the 2 p.m. movie.
快中午了而电影 2 点，所以先吃午餐。
Teacher Focus: 前句 + 后句 → pronoun reference → connector → time sequence → topic continuity.

Gap 4

Answer / 答案： G — After lunch, the girls headed towards the cinema.
Key clue / 关键： after lunch
Why? / 为什么？
G moves the story back to the cinema.
午餐后回电影院，G 最顺。
Teacher Focus: 前句 + 后句 → pronoun reference → connector → time sequence → topic continuity.

Gap 5

Answer / 答案： C — They bought a large popcorn to share.
Key clue / 关键： got popcorn
Why? / 为什么？
C explains what popcorn they bought.
前句说终于买到 popcorn，C 补充买了一大份分享。
Teacher Focus: 前句 + 后句 → pronoun reference → connector → time sequence → topic continuity.

Gap 6

Answer / 答案： D — There were many people at the cinema hall.
Key clue / 关键： cinema hall
Why? / 为什么？
D adds the next observation in the hall.
走向影厅后看到很多人，场景连续。
Teacher Focus: 前句 + 后句 → pronoun reference → connector → time sequence → topic continuity.
