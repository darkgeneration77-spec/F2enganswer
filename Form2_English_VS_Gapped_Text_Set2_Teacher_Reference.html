Form 2 English VS — Gapped Text Set 2

Moving House — Teacher’s Reference Version
Answer Key: 1E · 2H · 3A · 4C · 5G · 6B

Original Article / 原文

Lucas was excited because his family was moving to a new house and he would finally get his own room. He currently shared a room with his younger brother. 1. ______ He also would be getting his own room. After breakfast, everyone looked happy. 2. ______ Once it arrived, they moved the packed boxes to the truck. 3. ______ Then the family drove to the new house. Thirty minutes later, they arrived. 4. ______ The new house was much bigger and was double storey. 5. ______ There was also a balcony and a big garden. After the truck left, they arranged the furniture. 6. ______ Soon it was lunch time.

Options A–H

A. It took about an hour for all their things to be loaded into the moving truck.
B. They also unpacked some of the boxes.
C. The men from the moving truck then helped to load the things into the house.
D. Lucas's brother, Aiden liked his new room.
E. Lucas's brother, Aiden too, woke up feeling excited.
F. They also bought some new furniture.
G. It also has five bedrooms upstairs and a storeroom downstairs.
H. After breakfast, they waited for the moving truck to arrive.

Gap 1

Answer / 答案： E — Lucas's brother, Aiden too, woke up feeling excited.
Key clue / 关键： younger brother / He also
Why? / 为什么？
E introduces Aiden so the following “He also” has a clear reference.
E 先介绍 Aiden，后面的 He also 才有明确指代。
Teacher Focus: 前句 + 后句 → pronoun reference → connector → time sequence → topic continuity.

Gap 2

Answer / 答案： H — After breakfast, they waited for the moving truck to arrive.
Key clue / 关键： breakfast → truck
Why? / 为什么？
H gives the correct time sequence.
早餐后等待搬家卡车，时间顺序正确。
Teacher Focus: 前句 + 后句 → pronoun reference → connector → time sequence → topic continuity.

Gap 3

Answer / 答案： A — It took about an hour for all their things to be loaded into the moving truck.
Key clue / 关键： loaded boxes → leave
Why? / 为什么？
A completes the loading stage before the family leaves.
先装好东西，再出发，所以 A。
Teacher Focus: 前句 + 后句 → pronoun reference → connector → time sequence → topic continuity.

Gap 4

Answer / 答案： C — The men from the moving truck then helped to load the things into the house.
Key clue / 关键： arrived at new house
Why? / 为什么？
C logically follows arrival.
到新家后搬运工把东西搬进去。
Teacher Focus: 前句 + 后句 → pronoun reference → connector → time sequence → topic continuity.

Gap 5

Answer / 答案： G — It also has five bedrooms upstairs and a storeroom downstairs.
Key clue / 关键： house features
Why? / 为什么？
G continues describing the new house.
G 继续描述新房结构，与 balcony、garden 同主题。
Teacher Focus: 前句 + 后句 → pronoun reference → connector → time sequence → topic continuity.

Gap 6

Answer / 答案： B — They also unpacked some of the boxes.
Key clue / 关键： arranged furniture
Why? / 为什么？
B adds another unpacking task.
整理家具后拆箱，动作顺序合理。
Teacher Focus: 前句 + 后句 → pronoun reference → connector → time sequence → topic continuity.
