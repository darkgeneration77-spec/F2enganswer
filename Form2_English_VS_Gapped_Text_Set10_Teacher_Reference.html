Form 2 English VS — Gapped Text Set 10

Addicted Online Shopper — Teacher’s Reference Version
Answer Key: 1E · 2F · 3H · 4B · 5G · 6A

Original Article / 原文

My sister is an account executive in a company not far from our house. 1. ______ At least, she can accompany my mother at night. There is not much to do at home. 2. ______ She spends time chatting and visiting Facebook, where she started buying things from friends. 3. ______ Every day, deliveries arrive. She even buys things for mother. 4. ______ Mother starts asking her to stop buying. One day, mother had had enough. 5. ______ Mother demanded to see my sister's monthly budget and helped her prepare one. My sister now has only a small amount to spend. 6. ______ She wants my sister to realise that she must not spend unnecessarily.

Options A–H

A. Mother said that she is only helping my sister to save some money.
B. However, after some time, mother becomes annoyed with all the deliveries.
C. Mother wanted all my sister's money.
D. She scolded my sister and told her to stop buying things.
E. She stays at home and drives to her workplace which is about 20 km away.
F. Hence, after dinner, my sister will have all the time to go online.
G. She called my sister to sit in front of her.
H. Then she started to visit other websites and she got attracted to purchasing other things as well.

Gap 1

Answer / 答案： E — She stays at home and drives to her workplace which is about 20 km away.
Key clue / 关键： company not far from house
Why? / 为什么？
E explains she lives at home and can accompany her mother at night.
E 说明姐姐住家里，晚上能陪妈妈。
Teacher Focus: 前句 + 后句 → pronoun reference → connector → time sequence → topic continuity.

Gap 2

Answer / 答案： F — Hence, after dinner, my sister will have all the time to go online.
Key clue / 关键： nothing much to do
Why? / 为什么？
F gives the result that she has time to go online.
家里没什么事做，所以晚餐后有时间上网。
Teacher Focus: 前句 + 后句 → pronoun reference → connector → time sequence → topic continuity.

Gap 3

Answer / 答案： H — Then she started to visit other websites and she got attracted to purchasing other things as well.
Key clue / 关键： friends’ online business
Why? / 为什么？
H shows shopping expanding to other websites.
H 说明她后来浏览其他网站，越买越多。
Teacher Focus: 前句 + 后句 → pronoun reference → connector → time sequence → topic continuity.

Gap 4

Answer / 答案： B — However, after some time, mother becomes annoyed with all the deliveries.
Key clue / 关键： many deliveries
Why? / 为什么？
B introduces the mother’s annoyance.
送货越来越多，妈妈开始烦，所以 B。
Teacher Focus: 前句 + 后句 → pronoun reference → connector → time sequence → topic continuity.

Gap 5

Answer / 答案： G — She called my sister to sit in front of her.
Key clue / 关键： mother had enough
Why? / 为什么？
G gives the immediate action: she called the sister to sit down.
妈妈忍无可忍，下一步就是叫姐姐坐下来谈。
Teacher Focus: 前句 + 后句 → pronoun reference → connector → time sequence → topic continuity.

Gap 6

Answer / 答案： A — Mother said that she is only helping my sister to save some money.
Key clue / 关键： small amount to spend
Why? / 为什么？
A explains the mother’s goal of helping her save.
限制可花的钱，是为了帮姐姐储蓄。
Teacher Focus: 前句 + 后句 → pronoun reference → connector → time sequence → topic continuity.
