Vitamin, Minerals and Fibre · Teacher’s Reference Version

Original Text / 原文

Vitamins, minerals and fibre can be found in fruits and vegetables. In order to get your daily nutritional requirements and help your body fight diseases, you should include them in your diet every day.

Like cabbage, cauliflower and broccoli, they are all in the cruciferous family of vegetables including kale. Kale consists of characteristics such as having dark green leaves and a thick stalk. Kale can be either be places on top of a pizza as topping, steamed with olive oil and garlic or even just making a salad out of it.

Many nutritional benefits can be obtained from kale. You do not have to worry about the fat content when eating kale, because it doesn't have any fat! There are only 36 calories and 5 grams of fibre in one cup of kale. Therefore, kale is high in fibre and low in calories. Iron, calcium and vitamins K, A, and C are jam-packed in kale.

Besides that, antioxidants can also be found in kale. Removing potentially damaging oxidizing agents in the body is one of the many benefits that antioxidants offer. Flavonoids and carotenoids are antioxidants that are present in kale which help protect the body from cancers.

Fibre and sulphur are other useful substances found in kale. Not only do they keep the liver healthy, but they also help detoxify the body. If you would like to lower your cholesterol levels, you should eat kale regularly.
