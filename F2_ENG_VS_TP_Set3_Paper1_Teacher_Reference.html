Part 1 — Short Text / Multiple Choice (Q1–8)

Q1. What are the dates written on a time capsule?
A. The birth dates of the one sending and receiving.
B. The date the time capsule is prepared and buried.
C. The date the time capsule is buried and will be opened.
Answer / 答案： C — The date the time capsule is buried and will be opened.
Key clue / 关键： Date Buried + Do Not Open Until
Why? / 为什么？
The diagram shows the date buried and the future opening date.
图示直接给出埋下日期和未来开启日期。
Why not the others?
A: No birth dates are requested.
B: No separate preparation-date field is shown.
Teacher Focus： Read diagram labels directly.
Q2. The word they refers to
A. the last few centuries
B. the two streets
C. the writer's friends
Answer / 答案： B — the two streets
Key clue / 关键： Jalan Hang Jebat and Jalan Tan Cheng Lock
Why? / 为什么？
The pronoun refers to the two streets which had older street names.
they 回指前面两条街，因为后文说它们以前有其他名称。
Why not the others?
A: Centuries cannot have street names.
C: The friends were not known as Jonker and Herren Street.
Teacher Focus： Pronoun reference.

improved 直接说明空气质量正在改善。
Why not the others?

Q4. The sign means

B. Customers are requested to serve each other.
C. Customers have to serve themselves.

Key clue / 关键： DO NOT HANDLE THE FRUIT

handle 在这里表示触摸或拿取。
Why not the others?
B: Customers are not asked to serve each other.

Part 2 — Error Correction (Q9–16)

Q9. positiveness → ______
Answer / 答案： positivity
Key clue / 关键： noun form
Why? / 为什么？
The answer scheme uses the noun positivity.
答案表指定 positivity。
Q10. yourselves → ______
Answer / 答案： you
Key clue / 关键： pronoun
Why? / 为什么？
The answer scheme changes the pronoun to you.
答案表指定使用 you。
Q11. Have a talk → ______ a talk
Answer / 答案： having
Key clue / 关键： gerund as subject
Why? / 为什么？
The -ing form works as the subject phrase.
这里的动作短语作主语，所以用 having。
Q12. the easy way → the ______ way
Answer / 答案： easiest
Key clue / 关键： superlative
Why? / 为什么？
The + superlative gives “the easiest way”.
the 后面这里使用最高级 easiest。
Q13. possible to be solve → possible to be ______
Answer / 答案： solved
Key clue / 关键： be + past participle
Why? / 为什么？
Passive structure requires solved.
被动结构 be + past participle，所以用 solved。
Q14. Stress was to be used → Stress ______ to be used
Answer / 答案： is
Key clue / 关键： present tense
Why? / 为什么？
The passage gives general advice in the present.
整段是一般性建议，因此用 is。
Q15. will felt stressed → will ______ stressed
Answer / 答案： feel
Key clue / 关键： modal + base verb
Why? / 为什么？
After will, use the base verb feel.
will 后面接动词原形 feel。
Q16. There is many things → There ______ many things
Answer / 答案： are
Key clue / 关键： subject–verb agreement
Why? / 为什么？
Many things is plural, so use are.
many things 是复数，所以用 are。
