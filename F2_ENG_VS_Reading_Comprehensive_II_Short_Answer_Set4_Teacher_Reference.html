Set 4 — Shopping at Flea Markets

Teacher’s Reference Version ｜ English + 华语 explanation

Original Passage / 原文

Shopping at Flea Markets（跳蚤市场购物） Many youngsters（年轻人） enjoy shopping（购物） but may come up short of the funds（资金） to get the items they desire（渴望）. Therefore, instead of shopping at medium to high-end department stores（中高端百货公司）, youngsters consider shopping at flea markets（跳蚤市场） instead. Why should youngsters shop at flea markets? Shopping at a flea market resembles（类似于） going on a treasure hunt（寻宝）. The items may not be brand new（全新的） or the most durable（耐用的）, but you will get the best offers（最好的优惠）. However, you need to know a few things in order to shop at flea markets. First of all, you should try going early（早） on the first day（第一天） of the flea market to grab the best selections（选择）. Also, you should return again at the end of the last day（最后一天结束时） in order to get the biggest discounts（折扣）. If you want to go during the other days, you should be prepared to do some bargaining（讨价还价）. You should also consider getting a map（地图） of the flea market if there is one available（可获得的）. When you shop, you can easily mark（标记） the booths（摊位） where you left your paid purchases（购买的物品）. You can then pick the purchases up at a later time（之后）. Furthermore, you can also visit the booths that you missed（错过）, or would like to visit again later in the day. Remember to carry cash（现金） with you when shopping at the flea market. It is advisable（建议的） to take plenty of small bills（小钞票）. Most sellers（卖家） at flea markets do not take credit and debit cards（信用卡和扣账卡）. Besides, not every flea market has an ATM（提款机）. You should make sure to keep your cash in a front pocket（前口袋）. Avoid carrying a shoulder bag（肩包） that swings behind you, or a backpack（背包）. Flea markets can easily get crowded（拥挤） and you may get bumped（被撞） from time to time. A pickpocket（扒手） may bump you and use this as a cover（掩护） for pickpocketing（扒窃） your cash. If possible, take a foldable cart（可折叠推车） with wheels（轮子）. Then your hands will be free for browsing（浏览） as you buy things. You should also consider taking bubble wrap（气泡纸） or newspaper（报纸） for wrapping fragile items（易碎物品）. Although flea markets can be tiring（累人）, they can be exciting（令人兴奋的） too. You may find unique（独特的） and fabulous（极好的） items at very affordable prices（负担得起的价格）.
